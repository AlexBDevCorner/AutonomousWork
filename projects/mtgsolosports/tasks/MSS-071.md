# MSS-071 — Award Type Cup Final wildcards by qualification performance, not group number

## Goal

Remove the arbitrary qualification advantage given to lower-numbered Type Cup groups. In a Type Cup with 84 selected teams, the current three 28-team qualification groups advance 11/11/10 teams because the first two groups automatically receive the spare places. Change this to **10 guaranteed qualifiers per group, plus two wildcards awarded to the two best-performing 11th-placed teams**, so Group 3 has an equal chance to earn one of the final 32 places. Generalize the rule to all scalable Type Cup field sizes.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`; target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, branch `main`.
- User observed an 84-team Type Cup, with three equally sized qualifying groups (28 teams each). Existing format distributes the 32 Final places as 11/11/10, making Group 3's 11th place ineligible regardless of performance.
- Existing implementation:
  - `SimulationKernel/Cups/TypeCupTournamentFormat.cs`: `BalancedQualificationGroupSizes` and `AllocateFinalPlaces` give any extra Final places to larger groups, then lower group numbers.
  - `Features/Cups/DrawTypeCupQualificationGroups/DrawTypeCupQualificationGroupsHandler.cs`: persists group sizes and fixed `FinalPlacesForGroup` before any qualification rounds.
  - `Features/Cups/RunTypeCupTeam/TypeCupTournamentPlan.cs`: `SelectFinalists` currently takes exactly the persisted fixed quota from each group's ranked results.
  - `Features/Cups/RunTypeCupTeam/RunTypeCupTeamHandler.cs` and `PlayTypeCupTeamRound/PlayTypeCupTeamRoundHandler.cs`: build the fresh 32-team Final after qualifying, for full-run and step-by-step modes.
  - `Features/Cups/GetTypeCupTournament/GetTypeCupTournamentHandler.cs` and Type Cup Web pages: expose qualification groups, qualifiers and eliminated teams to the UI.
- MSS-061/MSS-062 established the scalable format and deterministic/resumable tournament. Do not regress it.
- Current tournament-format version and rules are persisted in saves. Past tournaments, drawn-but-unfinished tournaments and old single-field history must remain reproducible.

## Requirements

1. **Equal guaranteed quota plus performance wildcards.**
   - For `G = ceil(teamCount / 32)` qualifying groups, each balanced group receives `base = floor(32 / G)` guaranteed Final places. If `32 % G == 0`, all slots are guaranteed, with no wildcards.
   - There are `wildcardCount = 32 - G * base` extra places. Identify **one next-ranked candidate from each qualification group**, at group rank `base + 1` (or rank 1 when `base == 0`). Rank these candidates across groups by an explicit, stable sporting performance metric and advance the best `wildcardCount`. Thus no group can automatically receive an extra slot because of its number.
   - For 84 teams: three groups of 28; guaranteed top 10 in each group, and the two best among the three 11th-placed teams qualify, giving exactly 32 finalists.
   - Preserve the existing 32-team Final and exact-once inclusion of each selected team in qualification. No cross-group playoff and no additional competition rounds.

2. **Define comparable wildcard performance and ties.**
   - For **equal-sized groups**, compare the candidates' official cumulative qualification **team point totals**, highest first. Do not rank them by athlete selection rating, group number or strength estimates.
   - For **unequal group sizes**, normalize the accumulated team score to account for different expected base-scoring opportunities. Use a deterministic, documented calculation derived from the existing 32-place scoring table and the group's field size, e.g. compare the team total relative to the expected average base points per entrant (the `group size / sum(base points for positions 1..group size)` factor; common four-athlete and eight-round factors cancel). Explicitly explain the treatment of active career bonuses. Comparison must use exact integer/rational arithmetic, not floating-point rounding that can change qualifiers.
   - Define a deterministic, persisted/replayable cross-group tiebreaker for equal adjusted performances, not a default preference for Group 1/2 or alphabetical names. Follow existing RNG/tie-break conventions, preserving the same result and RNG-after across one-shot, stepwise, resume and replay.
   - Group standings and existing intra-group tiebreaks remain authoritative for determining each group's next-ranked candidate.

3. **Make qualifying status and history accurate.**
   - Distinguish guaranteed places, wildcard candidates, successful wildcards and eliminated teams in authoritative API/result models and UI. The draw must not misleadingly advertise 11/11/10 fixed quotas for a new-format 84-team edition.
   - Before all qualifiers finish, present guaranteed slots and *pending* wildcard competition honestly; do not display tentative wildcard teams as confirmed finalists.
   - Once qualification is complete, show which groups earned wildcard places and why (team scores / normalized comparison for unequal groups). Finalists list, badges, cutoff markers, Cup history, Live and completed replay must agree.
   - Preserve selection, nationality, medal/honour and historical rules: every athlete who actually participated in qualification stays permanently capped to their creature-type nationality, and official titles/podium medals come only from the Final.

4. **Versioning and compatibility (essential).**
   - Version the **per-edition qualification policy** or equivalent persisted tournament identity so a completed Cup replays with the exact format and qualifiers originally used. Do **not** reinterpret or rewrite old 11/11/10 results.
   - Any tournament with an already persisted old-format draw or played rounds must continue under its original quota/tiebreak rules and retain its results. Never silently redraw or resimulate.
   - **Future Type Cup editions in existing universes** must be able to use the fair wildcard policy; do not restrict the fix to brand-new saves merely because an existing save has a stored v1 rules snapshot. Upgrade/migrate safely at an edition boundary while preserving old persisted edition formats, and validate the outcome with an existing save that already has a completed Type Cup.
   - Preserve v0 legacy single-field and direct-Final <=32 behavior, and all existing deterministic save RNG and transaction/resumption invariants. Use safe schema migration if persisted metadata changes.

5. **Document the rule.**
   - Update `docs/game-rules.md`, relevant technical docs and player-facing explanatory text. Clarify that qualification points determine qualification/wildcards but never carry to the Final.

## Architectural direction

- Keep direct Endpoint -> Handler vertical slices, no MediatR. Reuse `TeamEvent` ranking and existing persisted qualification standings instead of inventing new competition scoring.
- Separate the group's *guaranteed* cutoff, any wildcard cutoff, and the final qualified set. A draw knows base quotas and the number of global wildcards; wildcard winners cannot be determined until every qualifier completes.
- Put quota/candidate selection and comparison in pure helpers, with rigorous tests. Persist policy version and enough wildcard/tie provenance to make read-only history and resumption deterministic.
- Avoid consuming sporting RNG from reads or replay. Preserve a single authoritative resolver used by both the one-shot runner and step-by-step runner.
- If the existing schema assumes fixed per-group quotas, evolve it deliberately rather than overloading an old-format field without version checks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] For 84 teams, qualification groups are 28/28/28; top 10 of each group qualify, and **exactly two of their three rank-11 teams** receive the remaining places based on performance. Any group's rank-11 candidate can be eliminated or advanced.
- [ ] Changing group numbers alone does not change who earns wildcard places when standings and draw-specific sporting state are equivalent.
- [ ] For 33-64 teams, the two groups still give 16 places each with zero wildcards; <=32 teams go directly to Final.
- [ ] Test 65, 83, 84, 85, 96, 97 teams and a larger field: group sizes stay <=32, each team has exactly one qualification group, guaranteed plus wildcard qualifiers total exactly 32, and no team appears in the Final twice.
- [ ] Wildcard selection uses official qualification team results, with a documented exact normalization for differing group sizes and deterministic unbiased tie resolution. Test equal-score ties and boundary cutoffs.
- [ ] In a qualifying Cup, status/API/UI show guaranteed and pending wildcard positions correctly; upon completion, they show exactly which candidates advanced and which were eliminated.
- [ ] Starting the Final before all qualification groups and wildcard resolution is forbidden. The Final's 32 teams start from zero and only Final standings award honours.
- [ ] One-shot vs next-round execution and save/reopen at qualification boundaries yield identical finalists, scores, tie outcomes, checksums and RNG-after state.
- [ ] Existing saves with a previously completed 11/11/10 Type Cup still show the original result, and their **next** Type Cup uses the new wildcard policy. Old draws with partially played rounds finish under their old policy.
- [ ] Legacy single-field, direct-Final, nationality, Cup history and full frontend/backend regression tests remain green.

3. Add integration tests of old-format persisted Cup results and in-progress old draws, then creation and execution of a subsequent new-format edition in the **same save**.
4. Verify GET/replay endpoints never alter RNG or results, and that a partial qualified state cannot prematurely show confirmed wildcards.
5. Demonstrate with a deterministic 84-team fixture in which the Group 3 rank-11 team outperforms a Group 1 or 2 rank-11 team and correctly takes a wildcard place.

## Out of scope

- Replacing the four athlete-rank groups x eight rounds competition format.
- A 1-v-1 elimination bracket, playoffs, wildcard tie-break matches or extra simulation rounds.
- Changing team squad selection, player nationality eligibility, career-bonus scoring, normal league scoring, Color Cup, or final-stage honours.
- Retroactive resimulation or rewriting of past Cup results.
- Unrelated refactoring of season lifecycle or autonomous orchestration.
