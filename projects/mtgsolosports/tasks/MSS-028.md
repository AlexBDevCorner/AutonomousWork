# MSS-028 — Integrate alternating post-season Cups into the season lifecycle

## Goal

Make odd seasons automatically run Color Cup and even seasons Type Cup at the correct post-rebalancing boundary.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`.
- Target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`.
- Default branch: `main`.
- Product rules: `docs/game-rules.md` in the target repository.
- Technical constraints: `docs/technical-design.md` and `AGENTS.md`.
- Architecture is vertical-slice modular monolith. **MediatR is forbidden.**
- Keep sporting simulation deterministic and use fixed-point integer arithmetic.

## Requirements

1. After league finalization/movement/rebalancing, schedule Color Cup for odd seasons and Type Cup for even seasons.
2. Expose Cup as explicit inspectable `Next Event` boundaries rather than silently simulating it before the UI can view the field/results.
3. Only after the required Cup is complete may the season finalize/decay and the next season start.
4. Cup selection/performance must use the completed season and current-season effective bonus values before season aging to the next year.
5. Fast-season/multi-season simulation must include the same Cup events and RNG/mathematics as manual progression.
6. Update dashboard/history navigation and story/honour projections for Cup outcomes.
7. Add end-to-end tests across at least three seasons proving Color/Type/Color rotation.

## Architectural direction

- Preserve existing state-machine legality and deterministic fast/manual equivalence.
