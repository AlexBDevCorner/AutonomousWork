# MSS-069 — Play and replay all 17 postseason qualifiers individually on Live

## Goal

Make every postseason qualifier in a three-division MtgSoloSports universe a real Live event, with its own 16-round playthrough, animated athlete reveal, cumulative standings, results and historical replay. Fix misleading mixed event/phase progress (for example, showing a completed Superleague qualifier as "Round 16 / 272"). Keep a convenient fast-forward for all remaining qualifiers without forcing players to watch 272 rounds.

## Context

- Target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, current `main`.
- Reproduction reported 2026-10-09 in a Season 2 postseason: Live displayed "Superleague qualifier complete", played rounds 1–16, but also "Round 16 / 272". The user could watch only the Superleague qualifier and could not find a way to reveal the feeder-to-feeder qualifiers on Live.
- Three-division saves have 17 independent 16-round qualifiers: one Superleague (32 athletes), eight F1↔F2 (16 athletes per color), eight F2↔F3 (16 athletes per color), i.e. 272 rounds in total.
- MSS-058 introduced persisted boundary/color qualifier identities, individual full-event execution, canonical order and `run-all`. MSS-060 added a Qualifiers overview/detail UI, but its promised individual *Live* reveal was not completed.
- Current backend `RunAllQualifiersHandler` executes remaining qualifier **events** in canonical order; `RunFeederQualifierEndpoint` executes an entire feeder event. Existing Superleague postseason endpoints already support playing its rounds individually.
- Current `PostseasonEvents.QualifierShape` reports all 272 rounds as one phase, while `HistoryEventRows` intentionally returns **only Superleague** rounds for the legacy `qualifier` event key. Frontend `LiveEventView` / `eventModel.ts` and Live routing likewise model `qualifier` as the Superleague event only.
- The existing `/saves/:saveId/qualifiers` overview and `/qualifiers/{boundary}[/{color}]` details display persisted final results but only offer instant execution for feeder events. Preserve their functionality.
- Relevant code to inspect (paths may evolve): `Features/Qualifiers/BoundaryQualifierRunner.cs`, `RunAllQualifiersHandler.cs`, `GetQualifierListHandler.cs`, `Features/Seasons/SeasonLifecycle/PostseasonEvents.cs`, `Features/History/EventRounds/HistoryEventRows.cs`, `MtgSoloSports.Web/src/features/qualifiers/QualifiersPage.tsx`, `features/live/LiveEventView.tsx`, `features/events/eventModel.ts`, `features/routing/routes.ts`, `features/dashboard/SeasonFlow.tsx`, `docs/game-rules.md`.

## Requirements

1. **Make qualifier identity explicit everywhere it matters.**
   - Represent an individual qualifier by source season + boundary + optional sporting color. Preserve the single mixed-color Superleague event and v1 single-feeder compatibility.
   - Distinguish an individual qualifier's 16-round progress from the phase-wide completion count (0–17 events, 0–272 total rounds). Never present phase-wide round count under an individual event title.
   - Use canonical order: Superleague, then the eight F1↔F2 qualifiers in sporting-color enum order, then eight F2↔F3 qualifiers in that order.

2. **Support backend per-round execution of feeder qualifiers.**
   - Add/refactor an endpoint/handler that persists exactly **one next round** of the selected feeder qualifier per request.
   - Use the existing deterministic simulation and versioned RNG. Persist authoritative round payload, checksum and RNG before/after. At round 16, finalize the selected event's standings and qualifying outcomes (top 8) exactly once.
   - Require a valid pending qualifier at the next canonical position; reject out-of-order events and attempts to replay already completed sporting rounds. Never silently advance a different qualifier.
   - Preserve the existing instant single-event and run-all APIs. These MUST resume unfinished qualifier rounds instead of replaying them, double-applying outcomes or breaking the RNG chain. Retrying a partially executed event or a run-all call must be safe.
   - Maintain current sporting invariants: Superleague 32 athletes / feeder 16; correct 8 qualifiers per event, proper movement, career bonus applied to scoring but no bonus or league championship points awarded, and no cross-boundary participant leakage.

3. **Provide per-event read models for Live and history.**
   - List persisted rounds and retrieve a selected round for a specific source season/boundary/color, including payload/placements, scoring and field metadata needed by the reveal.
   - Derive live progress and completed/pending state from persisted facts. Do not conflate the legacy Superleague history slice with feeder events.
   - Allow revisiting a completed feeder qualifier in an earlier postseason, with identical immutable playback and final standings. GET/replay must be read-only and must never consume RNG.

4. **Integrate all 17 competitions into the existing Live experience.**
   - Reuse `RoundReveal` / `LiveEventView` patterns rather than duplicating the UI for 16- and 32-athlete fields. Support Next Round, round navigation, Play/restart/reveal-all, and cumulative standings as cards are revealed.
   - For feeder events show boundary, sporting color, 8 upper-tier incumbents vs 8 lower-tier challengers, both source tiers and the **top-8 qualification cutoff**. Display correct athlete artwork, roles and result scores.
   - Each event shows `Round N / 16` (never `/ 272`). A separate phase overview may show `X / 17 qualifiers complete` and/or `Y / 272 total rounds`, clearly labelled as totals across the entire phase.
   - Keep distinct deep links/URL state for season, qualifier identity and selected round. Refresh, Back/Forward, navigation away/back and reopening history must preserve the right event without simulating anything.
   - Clear navigation: next legal qualifier after one finishes; previous/completed qualifiers to replay; return to qualifier overview/Dashboard. Do not make players visit the Dashboard between qualifiers.

5. **Preserve fast-forward and sensible entry points.**
   - From the qualifier overview and Live, let the user run all remaining qualifier rounds/events at once via the backend, respecting canonical order and skipping persisted completed work.
   - From qualifier detail, provide a **Play on Live** action for both Superleague and feeder qualifiers (including pending and completed events). Pending feeder events must no longer require instant completion just to become viewable on Live.
   - Dashboard may retain a single high-level qualifier lifecycle step, but its main play/view path must lead to the 17-event qualifier experience rather than present all 272 rounds as one 32-athlete Superleague event.
   - A complete phase advances to rebalance and subsequent postseason normally. No duplicate execution when following navigation or replay controls.

6. **Preserve accessibility, performance and compatibility.**
   - Controls work via keyboard; convey completion/cutoffs without color alone; honor reduced-motion preferences and current responsive layout.
   - Fetch one detailed qualifier at a time; no requirement to render 17 full athlete/round boards simultaneously.
   - Keep older single-feeder / v1 saves, completed historical Superleague qualifiers, Cups, movement reveals and other season phases working.
   - Update relevant docs/help copy so "one qualifier", "17 qualifiers" and "272 rounds" are never conflated.

## Architectural direction

- Prefer a shared qualifier event identity/read model and reuse of the current round-reveal stack and scoring/serialization logic.
- The current Superleague per-round runner and generic feeder full-event runner should converge on compatible progression semantics where practical; avoid 16 copied workflows.
- Backend is authoritative. Live UI never generates placements, RNG, outcomes or final sporting totals.
- Keep the project's direct Endpoint → Handler vertical slices, without MediatR or broad layered-architecture refactoring.
- Guard saved partial progress, canonical order, finalization and repeat calls with persisted invariants and per-save synchronization consistent with the current implementation.
- The existing `RunQualifier` lifecycle step can remain one phase-wide action, but its Live progress must not imply that all 17 events share one field or one 272-round match.
- Avoid changing established scoring and movement rules while fixing UX and execution granularity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In a tiered Season 2+ postseason the qualifier overview shows all 17 distinct events in the right order, with each event's state; the phase shows totals as phase-wide totals only.
- [ ] All eight F1↔F2 and all eight F2↔F3 events can each be opened on Live **before running them**, then played one round at a time with a 16-athlete animated reveal.
- [ ] The Superleague event retains its 32-athlete Live reveal. Every event shows its own `0..16 / 16` round progression rather than the confusing `16 / 272` mix.
- [ ] Each event has the correct boundary/color, incumbent/challenger origins, cumulative rankings, eight advancing athletes and visible cutoff.
- [ ] Finishing one qualifier offers a clear way to play the next one on Live without returning to Dashboard.
- [ ] Running all remaining qualifiers after any legal partial individual-round progress gives identical persisted sporting outcomes/checksums to the equivalent canonical per-round sequence from the same save/RNG state.
- [ ] Interrupted/retried single-round and run-all operations do not duplicate rounds, change completed events, corrupt RNG or apply movements twice.
- [ ] Out-of-order, already-finished and wrong-identity mutation requests fail clearly without changing save state.
- [ ] Completed feeder qualifiers can be revisited through stable URLs and replayed from saved rounds without new simulation.
- [ ] Dashboard, Qualifiers, Live and History do not suggest the Superleague 16-round match itself lasts 272 rounds.
- [ ] Existing v1 single-feeder saves, historical qualifiers and Cup/postseason progression still work.
- [ ] Automated backend and frontend tests cover round-by-round feeder progress, partial resume/fast-forward equivalence, identity, replay and UI navigation.
- [ ] Normal backend test suite and frontend test/typecheck/build pass.

## Out of scope

- Changing qualifier sporting rules, participant bands, promotion/relegation, scoring/bonus scales or the tier hierarchy.
- Introducing pool qualifiers or allowing movement across more than one league level in a postseason.
- Reworking Cup tournaments, global stage progression or movement/rebalance reveal designs.
- Building a separate 272-round mixed-athlete Superleague-like competition; these are 17 independent qualifiers.
- A redesign of the whole dashboard/navigation or a new general-purpose tournament framework.
- Recomputing or repairing old sporting results unless separately specified in a new task.
