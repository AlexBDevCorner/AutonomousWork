# MSS-104 — Award Chaos medals and 450-weight career credit

## Goal

Give every official Chaos entrant positive Career Leaders credit, and P1–P3 medals only.

## Context

- Project `mtgsolosports`, target `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports` (`main`). .NET + React, direct vertical-slice Endpoint → Handler, no MediatR; follow `AGENTS.md`. Approved Chaos Cup: 8 sporting colors, 1024 entrants (all source-season league athletes plus random Pool invitations), six 32-athlete group stages [32,16,8,4,2,1], top 16 from each nonfinal group; 16 game rounds per group, active bonus used but not earned; no league points/promotion, group scores reset; first draw exactly 4/color/group, later draws pure random. Runs after 32 league stages and before promotion qualifiers, only for a new season after deployment; odd/even Color/Type Cups keep alternating. Existing sporting history must remain unchanged.
- Scope: **Award Chaos medals and 450-weight career credit**. This is a single small PR, target roughly 200–600 changed lines where practical; if work expands materially, propose follow-up rather than bundling unrelated features.
- Dependencies: MSS-094, MSS-095, MSS-102; tasks with incomplete dependencies must wait.

## Requirements

1. Add individual Chaos category with base weight 450 versus Super 1000 and Color individual 800; champion roughly 675 career points, with tunable documented scoring curve.
2. Give same positive credit to every athlete eliminated at same stage and decreasing positive P1..P32 Final credit; Final P32 strictly exceeds a semifinal exit.
3. Record only Final gold, silver and bronze as official honours, no group-win title, no new bonus, promotions or league points.
4. Include Pool athletes' Chaos results in historic career points, without granting current Pool selection rights; avoid counting edition twice.
5. Version scoring and test old saves without Chaos contributions.

## Architectural direction

- Reuse existing feature/simulation/report patterns; keep deterministic integer calculations, save-scoped state and clear compatibility boundaries. Do not introduce MediatR or unrelated refactors.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 1024 completed entrants each have exactly one positive career credit and exactly three medals.
- [ ] Relative scores follow stage order; Career Leaders remains accurate and idempotent.

## Verification

`dotnet build MtgSoloSports.slnx --configuration Release` and `dotnet test MtgSoloSports.slnx --configuration Release` from target root. Add focused regression tests for the task's acceptance criteria and validate compatibility with prior editions. 

## Out of scope

- No Cup selection change, UI, or bonus effects.
- Do not implement any other task or modify unrelated history/data merely for convenience.
