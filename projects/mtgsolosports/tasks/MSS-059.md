# MSS-059 — Generalize feeder rebalancing into a tier cascade with pool access only at Feeder 3

## Goal

Adapt the current feeder/common-pool rebalance to the three-division pyramid.

Normal promotion/relegation and qualifier results must be applied first. Rebalancing then repairs structural color imbalance caused by the color-mixed Superleague while preserving 32 athletes in every F1/F2/F3 league. The common pool remains a balancing reservoir and may only enter/leave the competitive pyramid through Feeder 3.

## Context

- Depends on MSS-058.
- Current `RebalanceFeeders` handles one 32-athlete feeder per color after Superleague movement:
  - Superleague departures create feeder vacancies;
  - returning Superleague athletes can create feeder overflow;
  - overflow sends the lowest-ranked retained feeder athletes to pool;
  - shortfall draws random same-color pool athletes.
- With three feeder divisions, directly drawing pool athletes into F1 would bypass F2/F3 and undermine the pyramid.
- The desired separation is:
  - sporting performance controls normal adjacent-tier movement;
  - rebalancing only repairs population/color composition;
  - pool is not a league and has no normal relegation/qualification competition.

## Required rebalance semantics

After all automatic movement and all qualifiers are resolved, process each sporting color independently.

### A. Preserve earned movements

Athletes who just earned/received a competitive adjacent-tier result in this transition are protected from being immediately reversed by structural rebalance.

Examples:
- a newly promoted F2 athlete must not be immediately pushed back to F2 because Superleague returns made F1 temporarily overfull;
- a newly relegated athlete must not be immediately pulled back up by the same rebalance;
- Superleague returnees remain protected as in the current model.

### B. Cascade shortages downward

If F1 is below 32 after Superleague + normal F1/F2 movement:
1. pull the best eligible **retained** F2 athlete(s) upward to F1 until F1 is 32;
2. this creates F2 vacancies; pull the best eligible retained F3 athlete(s) upward to F2;
3. this creates F3 vacancies; fill only those F3 vacancies by deterministic equal-probability draws from the same-color common pool.

Use previous/source-season sporting rank as the primary deterministic ordering for retained-athlete structural moves, with existing deterministic tie conventions.

### C. Cascade overflow downward

If F1 is above 32:
1. move the lowest-ranked eligible **retained** F1 athlete(s) down to F2;
2. cascade any F2 overflow by moving its lowest-ranked eligible retained athlete(s) to F3;
3. only F3 overflow may displace athletes into the common pool.

Do not displace newly promoted/returned/protected athletes before retained athletes.

### D. Pool boundary

- Pool → active movement may only end in F3.
- Active → Pool movement may only originate from F3.
- Pool draw remains equal-probability among eligible same-color pool athletes and uses the shared versioned RNG.
- There is no cooldown; former league athletes may return in a later rebalance.
- Bonus continues aging while in pool.

## Requirements

1. Replace the one-feeder `RebalanceFeedersSelection` assumptions with a tier-aware per-color cascade.

2. Apply all competitive movement/qualifier results before structural rebalance.
   - Rebalance must not decide who won a promotion/relegation contest.
   - It only fills/relieves structural vacancies after those results.

3. End every rebalanced destination season with:
   - exactly 32 athletes in every F1 league;
   - exactly 32 in every F2 league;
   - exactly 32 in every F3 league;
   - exactly 32 in Superleague where applicable;
   - every remaining save athlete in the common pool;
   - no duplicates.

4. Pool must not bypass feeder levels.
   - No Pool→F1 or Pool→F2 movement rows.
   - No F1→Pool or F2→Pool movement rows.
   - Any color imbalance propagates through the feeder cascade until F3 is the only pool boundary.

5. Persist structural cascade movement explicitly.
   - Distinguish competitive promotion/relegation from structural rebalance-up/rebalance-down/pool-draw/pool-displacement.
   - Preserve source/destination league ids, feeder divisions, sporting color, source rank, and reason/provenance needed for history/UI.

6. Keep deterministic selection.
   - Upward structural refill chooses the best eligible retained source-season athletes deterministically.
   - Downward structural overflow chooses the lowest-ranked eligible retained athletes deterministically.
   - Only pool draws consume RNG, using same-color candidates and canonical color order.
   - Persist RNG after state atomically with pool draw results.

7. Protect earned movement.
   - Define and test the protected set for athletes who just changed tiers through automatic movement, qualifier outcome, inaugural Superleague creation, or Superleague return.
   - Rebalance should use retained athletes first and fail loudly if an impossible/corrupt roster cannot be repaired without violating invariants.

8. Support the inaugural Superleague transition.
   - When four athletes per F1 color enter the inaugural Superleague, the resulting F1 shortages should cascade F2→F1, F3→F2, then pool→F3.
   - Do not refill F1 directly from pool.

9. Update next-season roster/start invariants.
   - `StartNextSeasonInvariants`, `SeasonLifecycleEvaluator`, `EnsureRebalancedRosterAsync`, and response DTOs must validate all 24 feeder leagues, not eight.
   - Starting a season must be impossible while any feeder division is under/over 32 or a required qualifier/rebalance result is incomplete.

10. Generalize rebalancing result APIs.
    - Return enough information to reconstruct the full cascade without resimulation.
    - Group results by color and tier/boundary.
    - Historical reads must show the exact persisted athletes and movements.

11. Preserve current pool semantics.
    - Pool is used only when feeder color population is structurally too low/high after the competitive transition.
    - Do not turn pool rank/bonus/rating into a competitive selection mechanism.
    - Do not create normal F3 relegation to pool.

## Architectural direction

- Think of rebalancing as a deterministic per-color flow through F1 → F2 → F3 → Pool.
- Reuse the existing "retained candidate" concept and protection of newly returning athletes, but generalize it to all earned movement.
- Keep competitive movement and structural balancing as separate movement reasons; this distinction matters for history and sporting narrative.
- The algorithm should be testable as a pure selection/planning function before persistence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every feeder league ends the transition at exactly 32.
- [ ] Pool only connects directly to F3.
- [ ] F1 shortage cascades from F2, then F3, then pool.
- [ ] F1 overflow cascades to F2, then F3, then pool.
- [ ] Newly promoted/relegated/qualified/returned athletes are not immediately reversed by structural rebalance when retained alternatives exist.
- [ ] Structural upward/downward moves use deterministic source-rank ordering.
- [ ] Pool draws remain equal-probability and deterministic under the versioned RNG.
- [ ] Inaugural Superleague vacancies are filled by cascade, not direct pool→F1 draws.
- [ ] Start-next-season validation covers 24 feeder leagues plus Superleague.
- [ ] Movement history distinguishes sporting promotion/relegation from structural rebalance.
- [ ] Retry/reload is idempotent and never duplicates movement or consumes RNG twice.

## Verification

1. Run full backend tests.
2. Test no-color-change case: no structural rebalance beyond already resolved competitive movements.
3. Test a color heavily entering Superleague: F1 shortage cascades down and only F3 draws from pool.
4. Test a color heavily returning from Superleague: overflow cascades down and only F3 displaces to pool.
5. Test simultaneous shortages/overflows across several colors and deterministic RNG order.
6. Test protection of newly promoted/qualified/relegated/returned athletes.
7. Test inaugural Season 1→2 cascade.
8. Test final exact roster counts and global uniqueness.
9. Test that no persisted Pool↔F1/F2 movement exists.

## Out of scope

- Changing the competitive rank bands from MSS-058.
- Treating Pool as a league or qualifier.
- Strength-weighted pool draws.
- UI redesign of the rebalance reveal.
