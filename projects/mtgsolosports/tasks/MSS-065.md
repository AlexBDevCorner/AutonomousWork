# MSS-065 — Redesign Cup career prestige for Superleague, F1, F2, and F3

## Goal

Make the 10% career-prestige component of Cup selection represent the level at which achievements were earned.

A Feeder 3 title or stage win must remain meaningful, but it must not carry the same prestige as the equivalent Feeder 1 or Superleague achievement. Preserve Cup titles and Superleague appearances as major career signals.

## Context

- Depends on MSS-064.
- Current prestige is league-aware only in a coarse way:
  - feeder title = 100;
  - Superleague title = 300;
  - Superleague season appearance = 20;
  - every stage win/2nd/3rd = 10/5/2 regardless of league;
  - major Cup championship = 150.
- All feeder titles currently share `HonourKind.FeederTitle`.
- Stage podium prestige is currently calculated by simply counting all `StageStandingEntity.StageRank` 1/2/3 rows without checking their league.
- With F1/F2/F3 this would value an F3 championship exactly like F1 and an F3 stage win exactly like Superleague.
- Honours themselves should remain honours: this task changes prestige contribution, not whether a podium is recorded.

## Required semantic prestige values

Use these conceptual prestige values:

### League championship
- Superleague title: **300**
- Feeder 1 title: **100**
- Feeder 2 title: **50**
- Feeder 3 title: **25**

### Superleague participation
- completed Superleague season appearance: **20**

No generic F1/F2/F3 appearance prestige is added.

### League stage podium
Use F1's current values as the baseline:
- F1 stage 1st/2nd/3rd: **10 / 5 / 2**
- Superleague: **20 / 10 / 4**
- F2: **5 / 2.5 / 1**
- F3: **2.5 / 1.25 / 0.5**

### Major Cup championship
- Color Cup individual title: **150**
- Color Cup team title: **150**
- Type Cup team title: **150**

Cup runner-up/third-place remain honours but do not add prestige, matching current win-only prestige semantics.

## Requirements

1. Introduce an exact integer prestige unit scale capable of representing the fractional semantic values above without floating-point arithmetic.
   Recommended representation: **quarter-prestige-points** (`PrestigeScale = 4`).

   Under that representation:
   - Super title = 1200;
   - F1 title = 400;
   - F2 title = 200;
   - F3 title = 100;
   - Super appearance = 80;
   - Cup title = 600;
   - Super stage podium = 80/40/16;
   - F1 = 40/20/8;
   - F2 = 20/10/4;
   - F3 = 10/5/2.

2. Persist the prestige constants/model in the new versioned rules snapshot.
   - Existing v1 rules remain readable with their original integer prestige constants.
   - Future calculations on a tiered save use the new prestige model.
   - No floating-point or decimal sporting math.

3. Determine feeder-title prestige from the persisted league level of the honour's competition.
   - It is acceptable to keep `HonourKind.FeederTitle` as one honour kind if league id/level supplies the division.
   - Do not create separate title kinds solely to calculate prestige unless the domain model genuinely benefits.
   - Historical v1 feeder titles are treated as F1-equivalent achievements.

4. Determine stage-podium prestige from each `StageStandingEntity.LeagueId` and persisted league level.
   - Super/F1/F2/F3 stage podiums use the values above.
   - Never infer the level from current athlete membership; use the league where the stage was actually achieved.
   - Historical achievements keep their original league context permanently.

5. Preserve Superleague-appearance prestige at 20 semantic points per completed Superleague season.
   - Do not award appearance prestige merely for F1/F2/F3 participation.
   - Pool seasons add no appearance prestige.

6. Preserve Cup-title prestige at 150 semantic points per official championship.
   - Qualification-group wins from the scalable Type Cup are not major titles and add no prestige.
   - Type Cup Final champion remains the official Type Cup championship.
   - Runner-up/third-place honours remain prestige-neutral.

7. Replace the current count-only `PrestigeCounts` calculation with a shared league-aware prestige calculator usable by both Color Cup and Type Cup selection.
   - Avoid duplicated feeder/Super/stage counting logic in both selection handlers.
   - The calculator should operate from authoritative persisted achievements/league context and rules.

8. Keep career prestige cumulative with no time decay unless separately changed in a future rule.
   - A historical Superleague title remains prestigious.
   - This is intentionally different from effective bonus and recent form.

9. Keep the prestige component's outer Cup weight at **10%**.
   - Do not compensate for the new raw scale by altering 35/30/25/10.
   - Normalization removes uniform internal-unit scaling; the quarter-point representation is an exactness mechanism, not a weight increase.

10. Extend selection reports with an explainable prestige breakdown for shortlisted/selected athletes.
    At minimum report semantic or scaled contributions for:
    - league titles by level;
    - Superleague appearances;
    - stage podium prestige by level;
    - major Cup titles;
    - total prestige raw.
    Version report payloads and keep old versions readable.

11. Update docs/game rules to distinguish:
    - honours: top-three achievements shown in career history;
    - titles: first-place achievements;
    - prestige: a weighted career-selection input where competition level matters.

## Architectural direction

- Prefer a dedicated pure `CupPrestigeCalculator` with versioned constants over accumulating ad-hoc counts in each Cup handler.
- Preserve source league identity. An athlete currently in F3 can still carry prestige from earlier Superleague achievements.
- Do not rewrite historical Honour rows just to change their current prestige interpretation; derive from durable competition context.
- Fail loudly if an achievement that requires a league level references an unknown/corrupt league.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Super/F1/F2/F3 titles contribute 300/100/50/25 semantic prestige.
- [ ] Stage podiums use 2×/1×/1/2×/1/4× of the F1 10/5/2 baseline exactly.
- [ ] Fractional semantic values are represented with exact integer arithmetic.
- [ ] Superleague appearance remains 20 and no feeder appearance bonus is introduced.
- [ ] Major Cup championships remain 150.
- [ ] Cup 2nd/3rd and Type Cup qualification-group wins add no prestige.
- [ ] An athlete's old Superleague achievements retain their higher prestige after later relegation.
- [ ] Historical v1 feeder achievements are treated as F1-equivalent in the tiered prestige model.
- [ ] Color Cup and Type Cup use one shared prestige calculation.
- [ ] Outer prestige weight stays 10%.
- [ ] Selection reports explain where prestige came from.
- [ ] Existing v1 snapshots/reports/results remain readable.

## Verification

1. Run the full backend suite.
2. Add pure prestige tests for every title/stage-podium/appearance/Cup value.
3. Test mixed-career examples, including:
   - one Superleague title vs three F1 titles;
   - F1 vs F2 vs F3 champions;
   - historical Superleague star currently in F2/F3;
   - athlete with many lower-tier stage podiums vs a smaller number of upper-tier podiums.
4. Verify quarter-point exactness for F2/F3 2nd/3rd stage values.
5. Verify Cup runner-up/third-place do not change prestige.
6. Verify Type Cup qualification-stage success alone does not change prestige.
7. Verify Color/Type Cup produce identical prestige raw for the same athlete history.
8. Verify report prestige breakdown sums exactly to persisted/reported prestige raw.

## Out of scope

- Changing what counts as an honour.
- Adding feeder participation prestige.
- Prestige decay over time.
- Changing Cup outer weights.
- Adjusting performance/form factors from MSS-064.
