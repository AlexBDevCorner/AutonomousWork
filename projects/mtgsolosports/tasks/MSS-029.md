# MSS-029 — Add Eurovision-style animated round reveal and replay

## Goal

Deliver the nostalgic spectator experience where card standings visibly move as an already-computed round is revealed.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`.
- Target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`.
- Default branch: `main`.
- Product rules: `docs/game-rules.md` in the target repository.
- Technical constraints: `docs/technical-design.md` and `AGENTS.md`.
- Architecture is vertical-slice modular monolith. **MediatR is forbidden.**
- Keep sporting simulation deterministic and use fixed-point integer arithmetic.

## Requirements

1. Animate the immutable backend presentation result rather than triggering incremental sporting simulation.
2. Provide at least instant and animated reveal modes plus reasonable speed/pause controls.
3. Show card artwork/fallback, awarded points and movement in cumulative stage standings as the reveal progresses.
4. Use the same component/presentation model for a live completed round and historical round replay.
5. Refreshing, changing speed, pausing or replaying must never consume RNG or alter persisted results.
6. Keep animation state local to React.
7. Respect reduced-motion browser preference with an accessible instant/minimal-motion alternative.
8. Add component/state tests where practical and preserve frontend typecheck/build.

## Architectural direction

- Do not use animation callbacks to mutate sporting state or call simulation endpoints multiple times.
