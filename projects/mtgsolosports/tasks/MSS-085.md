# MSS-085 — Add league profile pages with seasonal podium history, league records, and all-time points leaders

## Goal

Create a dedicated **Leagues** section in MtgSoloSports, analogous in discoverability and visual quality to the existing **Cups** section, so each league has its own historical profile. Users should be able to explore the medalists of every completed season, notable records belonging specifically to that league, and an all-time leaderboard by accumulated official league championship points. Make the simulated sporting history feel alive, discoverable, and coherent.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`; target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, default branch `main`.
- Requested 2026-10-24.
- Existing navigation includes Cups and Standings; the user wants an analogous destination focused on leagues, not just a season selector for current standings.
- A universe may contain **Superleague**, **Feeder 1**, **Feeder 2**, **Feeder 3**, possibly multiple distinct divisions at a tier, and older/legacy universes with a different set of levels. Read the actual league identity and history model instead of hard-coding assumptions.
- Hall of Fame > Career Leaders uses a newly introduced **weighted career performance score**, combining league tiers and major cup achievements. **That is a separate statistic.** A league page's all-time points must use actual completed **championship/standings points earned in that league**, not weighted Career Leaders points, active bonuses, MTG card stats, or Cup results.
- The central design goal is an emergent, believable sporting ecosystem with a meaningful history: distinguish dynasties, one-season peak performances, consistency and records.

## Requirements

1. **Leagues navigation and league selection**
   - Add a first-class **Leagues** destination consistent with the existing Cups navigation, page organization, dark-theme design and responsive layout.
   - Provide a list/overview of leagues available in the selected universe, with an easy way to open a **dedicated page/profile for each league**. Display league name, tier and relevant summary (e.g., most recent completed champion, seasons with history) when supported by stored data.
   - Include Superleague and each distinct feeder league/division present in the current universe; do not conflate leagues that share a tier but have separate identities. Do not present Pool as an official league unless it really has the same official recorded season standings/awards model.
   - Use stable league IDs (rather than array indices or mutable display names) for URLs/selection and tie all data to the selected universe. Support deep links, browser back/forward, refresh, and changing universes without leaking another universe's data.
   - Follow existing navigation and presentation conventions in Cups / Standings rather than building an isolated visual system.

2. **Season-by-season podium history**
   - Show a clear historical list/table of every **completed league season**, newest first by default, identifying **champion, runner-up and third place** with season labels and medal presentation.
   - Athlete names should link to the existing athlete profile; optionally link each season to its existing detailed Standings view for that exact season and league.
   - Correctly handle ties according to official league ranking rules; don't fabricate a third-place athlete where fewer than three valid placements exist. Where supported, show the winning athlete's points and/or winning margin.
   - Do not present ongoing/provisional standings as final medalists. If useful, show the current ongoing season in a separate clearly marked panel, but never count it in historical awards or records.
   - Handle athletes no longer active or moved to other leagues: their historical achievements must remain credited to the exact league and season where they occurred.

3. **League-specific all-time points leaderboard**
   - Show the top athletes ranked by **the sum of their official final season standings/championship points earned while competing in the selected league**, across **all completed seasons in that league**. Repeated spells in a league must count.
   - Use the authoritative final **per-athlete, per-league, per-season total** exactly once; do **not** add individual round/stage points on top of a final total or accidentally aggregate duplicate snapshots. Verify from existing persistence what 'points' means in Standings, and use the same statistic and precision.
   - Explicitly label this value `Total league points` / `Total points in this league`. It must **not** be the new weighted Hall of Fame career score, current bonus, Color/Type Cup points, raw per-stage awards already included in season totals, or an athlete's results in another feeder tier / Superleague.
   - Include meaningful supporting columns such as completed seasons competed, best league finish, league titles and/or podiums, with clear deterministic ordering/tie-breaks.
   - Show a useful top slice with an option to inspect more/full standings if practical (pagination or existing table patterns); never silently restrict all-time calculation to only currently active athletes or one season.

4. **Dedicated league records**
   Provide a polished **Records** section grounded exclusively in completed official results **from the selected league**, with the record holder(s), value, and season(s) or relevant date range where applicable. At minimum include:
   - **Most league championships** (career within the selected league).
   - **Most podium finishes** (gold/silver/bronze in the selected league).
   - **Most league appearances / completed seasons** (consistency).
   - **Highest single-season points total** (individual performance record, with season).
   - **Longest consecutive championship streak** (dynasty; by successive completed seasons of this league; don't treat missed seasons as consecutive).
   - **Largest championship winning margin** (champion minus runner-up in a completed season when comparable data exists).

   Add further informative records only when safely derivable and meaningful, such as most runner-up finishes, most top-10 finishes, most top-3 consecutive finishes, or highest average season points among athletes with a stated minimum number of league seasons. Avoid a cluttered dashboard of misleading records.
   - Define rules for equal values/tied record holders, eligibility, historical formats, and missing information. Display ties honestly rather than choosing an arbitrary winner.
   - Never equate weighted Career Leaders points with actual league championship points; don't infer a record from current temporary performance bonuses.
   - If formats, participant counts or season scoring scales have changed, make raw record comparisons transparent; omit records that can't be compared fairly or clearly label their scope/assumptions.

5. **Narrative and presentation**
   - Include a compact header/identity area for each league (name/tier, number of completed seasons, unique historical participants, total awarded championships if derivable).
   - Optionally feature the latest champion and a `Legends of this league` / dynasty highlight based on factual career-in-this-league titles, streaks or consistent results. Do not produce invented prose or fabricated achievements; use deterministic data-driven labels.
   - Keep league pages visually coherent with Cups, Hall of Fame and athlete pages, favoring easy scanning over excessive dense tables. Ensure mobile/narrow-screen layouts avoid horizontal overflow; loading, no-history and error states must be usable.
   - Make league profiles discoverable from appropriate existing league names/headings in Standings if this is a small, low-risk change, without changing Standings' main behavior.

6. **Data integrity, architecture and performance**
   - Inspect the existing domain/API/frontend and identify the authoritative persisted **completed final league standings** before building aggregation; distinguish historical league identity from current athlete affiliation and tier labels.
   - Build shared backend projections/query handlers for league profiles, podium history, league all-time rankings and records, reusing existing endpoint → handler patterns. Frontend must not reconstruct historical results by fetching every season separately if a backend aggregate is appropriate.
   - Recalculate from historical stored results on read; do not alter simulation rules, bonus award formulas or recorded results. Prevent N+1 queries and expensive repeated database work on leagues with many seasons/athletes.
   - Keep a consistent definition of completed/official results across all panels. Gracefully handle older saves, missing league tiers/divisions, fewer than three athletes, unknown final scores, ties, and empty universes. Do not invent missing data.
   - Do not overwrite or redefine the existing Hall of Fame Career Leaders ranking or the Standings view.

## Architectural direction

- Review current **Cups** routes/navigation/components and **Standings** season/league selection; reuse established cards, tables, selectors and athlete links where appropriate.
- Trace authoritative league-season final results via existing read models/persistence, resolving league identity across historical seasons and tier/division changes; ensure aggregation keys cannot mix separate feeders or duplicate round snapshots.
- Prefer backend `Leagues overview` and `League details` query endpoints that return clearly typed summary, podium list, all-time records and rankings. Aggregate once or in efficient grouped queries rather than per-athlete/per-season HTTP fan-out.
- Keep the source of each record auditable (athlete, league ID, season, points/placement); introduce small deterministic pure aggregators and test them thoroughly.
- Respect the project's vertical-slice approach (Endpoint → Handler; no new MediatR dependency).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A dedicated **Leagues** section exists alongside Cups, with browsable profiles for Superleague and all distinct official feeder leagues present in a universe.
- [ ] Each league profile shows a season-by-season **gold/silver/bronze history** based only on finalized results, with links to athlete profiles.
- [ ] Each league profile shows an all-time **Total league points** leaderboard summing final official season points earned in that *specific league*, across completed seasons, without double counting or including Cup / Career Leaders / active bonus data.
- [ ] Records include titles, podiums, appearances, highest single-season points, consecutive titles and largest winning margin wherever authoritative data supports them; holders, values and season context are correct.
- [ ] The user can navigate directly to a league profile, refresh it, navigate back, and switch universes without stale data or wrong-league results.
- [ ] Promotion, relegation, inactivity or later league changes do not retroactively reassign achievements; distinct feeder divisions are not merged.
- [ ] Unfinished seasons don't affect medals, official records or all-time point totals.
- [ ] Equal records and missing/incomparable results are handled explicitly; no fabricated scores/medalists.
- [ ] League pages look consistent with Cups, work on desktop and mobile, and have useful empty/loading/error states.
- [ ] Backend aggregation tests, frontend UI tests and existing project build/test suites pass; competition mechanics and persistence remain unchanged.

## Verification

1. Use a representative saved universe with multiple completed seasons, Superleague and Feeder 1/2/3 (where present). Check overview, per-league routes and all completed seasons' podiums against historical Standings.
2. Manually verify at least two known long-career athletes with promotions/relegations and multiple spells: their total league points match the sum of **final Standings points for that league only**; cross-league and Cup points do not leak.
3. Add focused aggregation tests for a three-season dynasty, interrupted reign, a tied record, shared title counts, repeat appearances, a competitor changing leagues, and a league with fewer than three competitors.
4. Test peak-season record and championship winning margin using controlled final score fixtures; verify no provisional or incomplete results are included and no stage-total duplication occurs.
5. Test distinct same-tier feeder divisions and legacy universes with missing tiers. Verify old records do not crash and that missing or genuinely incomparable data is explicitly omitted/labeled.
6. Verify deep links, back/forward navigation, athlete navigation, universe switching, loading/empty/error states, and narrow mobile viewport behavior.
7. Run the existing `MtgSoloSports` .NET and frontend test/type-check/build commands appropriate to the repository; include screenshot(s) and a concise data-source/record-definition explanation in the PR.

## Out of scope

- No changes to league simulation, schedules, points awarded, promotion/relegation, bonuses, athlete cards, or historical standings.
- No recalculation or retroactive modification of official sporting results.
- No changes to Color Cup / Type Cup formats, records or pages.
- No changes to weighted Hall of Fame Career Leaders scoring/formula.
- No cross-universe global leaderboard or external data aggregation.
- No AI-generated commentary, speculative narrative, or new complex historical analytics engine beyond the requested league profiles and records.
