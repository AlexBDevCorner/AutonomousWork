# MSS-080 — Highlight qualifying positions in live Type Cup standings

## Goal

Make the live Type Cup easier to follow by visually distinguishing teams currently inside the qualification cutoff in each qualification group's live standings. Players should be able to see immediately which teams would advance to the Final if the group ended at that moment, without counting rows manually.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`; target repo: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, branch `main`.
- Requested 2026-10-13 after improvements to the multi-stage Type Cup live reveal.
- In **Live > Type Cup > Qualification Group A**, the **Team standings in Qualification Group A** table shows ranked teams with columns such as Team, This round and Points. All places currently look visually identical. The screenshot shows Qualification Group A at Round 15/32, Squad #2, Round 7/8.
- The request is a small UI improvement: if the top 10 in a group qualify, the top 10 positions should be highlighted. **Ten is an example, not a fixed rule.** The actual number of Final slots can vary by group and tournament format.
- This concerns provisional *team* ranks in the live standings table, not athlete card positions in the animated reveal.

## Requirements

1. **Use the actual qualification cutoff.**
   - Determine the number of qualifying teams for the current Type Cup qualification group from the authoritative tournament configuration/progression logic; do not hard-code 10 or assume every group has an equal number of slots.
   - For each qualification group, highlight exactly the currently qualifying ranks according to the applicable advancement rules, including any cross-group best-runner-up or tie-break allocation rule. If advancement depends on results from other groups not yet settled, distinguish guaranteed qualifying places from conditional/contested places rather than incorrectly implying guaranteed qualification.
   - The cutoff must remain correct for different numbers of selected teams, qualification groups and Final places (e.g. a 32-team Final), including uneven allocations between groups.

2. **Show the boundary clearly in the existing live team standings.**
   - Apply a subtle, theme-consistent visual treatment (such as a muted background tint or narrow accent border) to rows currently in qualifying positions.
   - Make the boundary clear between the last qualifying and first nonqualifying position, without overwhelming team names or numerical scores.
   - Include a small explanation/legend such as `Top N currently qualify` or `N automatic places`; distinguish provisional and confirmed results as appropriate. Do not rely on color alone to explain qualification.
   - Keep the existing table columns, ranking, team score values and general compact layout intact.

3. **Update as live results change.**
   - During live round-by-round reveal, update the highlighted rows when provisional rankings change, including replay, restart, step-forward/back and completing a group.
   - The qualification cue follows the current group and stage; it must not leak Group A's cutoff into Group B, Group C or the Final.
   - In the Final (no further qualification cutoff), do not misleadingly mark the top teams as 'qualifying'; retain normal Final standings/podium behavior.

4. **Preserve competition behavior.**
   - This is presentation only: no changes to advancement logic, scores, persisted rounds, qualifier scheduling or Cup outcomes.
   - Reuse existing ranking/qualification data where possible; if the UI lacks the required cutoff, expose only the minimal authoritative information needed rather than recreating a competing rules engine in React.

## Architectural direction

- Locate the existing Type Cup live **Team standings in Qualification Group ...** rendering and the data that establishes per-group Final qualification slots.
- Prefer a localized frontend change, with a narrowly scoped API/read-model extension only if necessary.
- Maintain readability in the dark theme and narrow viewports. Ensure table highlighting remains legible with live ranking movements.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] During Qualification Group A, current automatic qualifying places are visually distinct from nonqualifying places in the **team standings** table, and a label explains the cutoff.
- [ ] For tournaments with uneven Final-slot allocation between groups, each group shows its own correct qualifying threshold; nothing assumes a constant top 10.
- [ ] Conditional cross-group qualification or tie-break cases are not falsely presented as guaranteed qualification.
- [ ] When team positions change during round reveals or replay, the highlight follows the current provisional ranking rather than stale team identities.
- [ ] Navigating among qualification groups and Final never shows the prior group's qualification highlight or misleading qualification labels.
- [ ] Existing table layout, scores, round controls and Type Cup results remain unchanged apart from the targeted qualification cues.
- [ ] Focused tests cover the qualification boundary, differing slot counts, stage changes and live-ranking updates.

## Verification

1. Inspect the current frontend project scripts, then run its existing frontend tests, type-check and build as appropriate.
2. Add or update focused rendering tests around the live Type Cup team standings: qualifying rank count, first nonqualifying rank, rank changes, per-group different quota, and Final with no qualifying highlight.
3. Manually review an in-progress multi-group Type Cup in the Live view, including a group change and replay, in desktop and narrow layouts.

## Out of scope

- No redesign of the Type Cup sidebar, stage navigation, animated reveal cards or tournament controls.
- No change to qualification algorithms, tie-breakers, competition formats or Final size.
- No changes to general league standings, athlete profiles or other tournament screens.
- No broad refactoring of the Live view.
