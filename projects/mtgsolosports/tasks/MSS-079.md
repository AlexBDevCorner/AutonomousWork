# MSS-079 — Fix Type Cup completed-state mismatch and ambiguous round replay

## Goal

After finishing a multi-stage Type Cup, make **Live, Dashboard, Cups and backend season-status agree that the tournament is complete**, stop displaying playable Cup controls, and allow safe replay of persisted rounds. Fix the `Sequence contains more than one matching element` exception caused by ambiguous round identity across separate Type Cup qualification groups and the Final. A completed Season 4 Type Cup must lead to the actual next legal action (**Start Season 5**), not another `Run all rounds` suggestion.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`; target repo: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, branch `main`.
- Reported 2026-10-12 for one save after using **Run remaining rounds** because the live multi-qualification reveal was too confusing (related UX request: `MSS-078`). The season is **Season 4**, a Type Cup with **83 selected teams** and **three qualification groups plus a 32-team Final**.
- After the bulk completion, **Live** shows `Type Cup — team complete` and `Pick a round to replay it` but also shows `The round did not complete: Sequence contains more than one matching element`; its tournament pane marks qualification groups A/B/C and Final `COMPLETED`, while still displaying the obsolete `Current stage: Qualification Group 2 of 3`.
- **Cups** recognizes Season 4 as **Completed**, awards **Eldrazi Gold, Rogue Silver, Elemental Bronze**, and includes that edition in all-time Cup stats.
- **Dashboard** still highlights `Cup: team — Group 4 · Round 8 / 8`, offers `Play on Live` and **Run all rounds**, and does not mark the Cup step as done or offer **Start Season 5**. Never silently invite a repeat of a committed Cup.
- Repositories inspected: `src/MtgSoloSports/Features/History/EventRounds/HistoryEventRows.cs` orders Type Cup rounds by `TournamentPhase` and `QualificationGroup` but projects them to `StoredRound(GroupNumber, RoundNumber, ...)`, **discarding both stage fields**. `GetHistoryEventRoundHandler.cs` then calls `rows.SingleOrDefault(r => r.Group == group && r.Round == round)`. These coordinates repeat per qualification group and Final: a definite non-unique lookup and strong explanation for the observed exception.
- `ListHistoryEventRoundsHandler` and frontend `eventsApi.ts`/`LiveEventView.tsx` similarly represent rounds only by `group` and `round`, including URL and `revealKey`, so they cannot uniquely address distinct Type Cup stages. `GetHistoryEventTeamStandingsHandler.ProjectProvisionalAsync` also applies `(beforeGroup, beforeRound)` filtering across all stages, risking incorrect replay-era provisional team totals.
- Backend lifecycle `CupExtensionPoint.LoadCupStateAsync` already checks for persisted Type Cup **Final** (or legacy) standings and `SeasonLifecycleEvaluator.SnapshotCupComplete` should offer `StartNextSeason`. `RunTypeCupTeamHandler.EnsureTournamentUnresolvedAsync` rejects a new run if Final standings exist. Thus Dashboard discrepancy needs root-cause confirmation using persisted state / status and frontend refresh, rather than assuming the completion operation failed.

## Requirements

1. **Reproduce using a completed multi-group Type Cup.**
   - Cover Season 4 with 83 teams / three qualifiers and Final, both incremental live rounds followed by bulk completion and wholly bulk completion.
   - Inspect persisted `TypeCupTeamRounds` with their full stage keys, Final team/leg standings, medals/honours, save phase and `GET /season-status` response. Determine whether the screenshot's Dashboard problem comes from stale frontend status, incorrect backend lifecycle flags/progress, partial persistence, or a combination.
   - The already-completed save must be recoverable non-destructively without rerunning the Cup or requiring a new universe.

2. **Make stored Type Cup round identity unique and stage-aware.**
   - Preserve `TournamentPhase` and `QualificationGroup` alongside `GroupNumber` (squad rank group) and `RoundNumber` in list/detail API models and lookups. Distinguish legacy/direct Final paths as appropriate.
   - Update `HistoryEventRows`, `ListHistoryEventRounds`, `GetHistoryEventRound`, frontend `eventsApi` and `LiveEventView` to round-trip stage identity. Do not use `FirstOrDefault` as a workaround for duplicate keys: it would return the wrong persisted stage.
   - Keep older `Color Cup`, `Superleague qualifier` and feeder qualifier replay contracts working; add compatible optional parameters or a Type Cup-specific endpoint if required.
   - Fix stage-scoped before-round standings/reveal baselines and playback keys, so a qualification group's scores/replay cannot accidentally include another qualification group or Final, and Final totals start fresh at zero.
   - Repair the misleading generic `group 4 round 8 / 8` and `Qualification Group 2 of 3` stale labels after complete, including replay selection/state reset.
   - Show a reliable error or empty replay state when a requested stage+round is not persisted, instead of surfacing internal LINQ exceptions.

3. **Reconcile completion across Live, Dashboard and Cups.**
   - Once the Final and authoritative completion invariants are persisted, `GET /season-status` must report `cupTeamResolved=true`, `cupComplete=true`, `readyToStartNextSeason=true` and `legalNextActions=['StartNextSeason']` (assuming the other postseason prerequisites already shown complete in the reported save).
   - Dashboard must mark **Cup: team** done, stop presenting `Run all rounds`/`Play on Live` as playable actions, and show **Start Season 5** as the next step. Live must show completion/replay and not suggest simulation of an already completed Cup.
   - On a completed bulk action, refresh backend authoritative state across navigation, Back/Forward and reload; do not keep the old event progress or `lastStage` cursor. Handle failed or late refresh explicitly rather than confidently showing stale controls.
   - If a completed Final exists but lifecycle reports the Cup unfinished, fix the underlying projection/inconsistency with an idempotent non-destructive reconciliation. Do not merely force frontend stepper labels; never bypass required backend preconditions.
   - If actual persistent records are inconsistent, surface a diagnostic/recovery explanation and fail safely rather than duplicating sporting results.

4. **Guard against duplicate execution and save corruption.**
   - Confirm server endpoints used by both Dashboard and Live reject re-execution of an already completed Cup without altering `TypeCupTeamRounds`, standings, honours, athlete bonuses, RNG, or save phase.
   - Ensure repeated clicks, route reloads, stale clients and simultaneous calls cannot rerun completed Final rounds or duplicate medals. Show a useful `Already complete` message and the next legal navigation, not an internal exception.
   - Preserve the correct Season 4 champion **Eldrazi** and its podium **Rogue / Elemental** on the reported save, and keep completed history and statistics stable.

## Architectural direction

- Fix the data contract/identity loss at the source rather than suppressing exceptions in React. Stored Type Cup rounds already contain `TournamentPhase` and `QualificationGroup`; use these to disambiguate stage-local `GroupNumber + RoundNumber`.
- Audit both `HistoryEventRows.LoadAsync` and `GetHistoryEventTeamStandingsHandler.ProjectProvisionalAsync`, plus all API callers/replay links. Check `EventRoundView`/DTO backward compatibility and `LiveEventView` caching and selection comparisons.
- Audit `RunTypeCupTeamHandler.CompleteAsync`, `CupExtensionPoint.LoadCupStateAsync`, `SeasonLifecycleEvaluator`, `PostseasonEvents.WithTypeCupProgressAsync`, `GET /season-status` and `SeasonFlow`/Dashboard refresh. Preserve backend authority for stage progression and finalization.
- Keep this task focused on correctness, recoverability and end-to-end progression. `MSS-078` owns the broader redesign into distinct competition workspaces; coordinate shared round-identity work without duplicating its UX scope.
