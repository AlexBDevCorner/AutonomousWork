# MSS-074 — Skip the transient league-complete screen after fast-forward

## Goal

When the player fast-forwards the remaining league season, move directly to the **Promotion & relegation** step as soon as the league season is complete. Do not briefly show an intermediate **League season complete** panel. Remove the associated four-button action bar from the Dashboard season-flow UI.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`; target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, branch `main`.
- Reported 2026-10-11. Reproduction: start league play and fast-forward the full league season. For a couple of seconds, Dashboard still highlights **League play** and shows a **League season complete** summary, then advances to **Promotion & relegation**.
- The temporary panel reports "Completed 32 stages (global stage 1 → 33)", prompts the player to inspect final tables, and says postseason actions are pending. It includes a four-button action bar: **Inspect final tables**, **Replay history**, **Inspect Cups**, and **Refresh status**. This screen is distracting because it vanishes by itself shortly afterward.
- The player wants to proceed straight to the next lifecycle step, without that interim panel or its four-button bar. The league results and postseason flow are already available elsewhere in the UI.
- This is a presentation/navigation problem. Completing league play must **not** automatically execute promotion/relegation or skip any required postseason action.

## Requirements

1. **Go directly to the next phase when fast-forward finishes.**
   - After the last league stage has been persisted and the authoritative season status confirms league completion, transition Dashboard season flow directly to **Promotion & relegation** (or the actual next legal postseason step returned by the backend).
   - Do not render **League play** as the active step with a temporary **League season complete** message while waiting for an eventual status refresh.
   - Avoid flicker or a short-lived stale screen caused by competing local completion flags, cached status, loading states, delayed polling or out-of-order responses.
   - Refresh/invalidate relevant season state promptly and consistently after the fast-forward mutation resolves. If the refresh is pending, prefer a stable loading/transition state over incorrect league-play content.

2. **Remove the redundant completed-league action component.**
   - Remove the **League season complete** intermediate panel from the normal completed-league Dashboard flow, including its entire four-button row: **Inspect final tables**, **Replay history**, **Inspect Cups**, **Refresh status**.
   - Do not merely hide the panel with a timer; eliminate the redundant rendered state and any now-unused component code for it.
   - Preserve access to final tables, history, Cups and refresh through existing, appropriate navigation elsewhere. Do not delete those features or remove buttons belonging to other screens.

3. **Preserve authoritative postseason progression.**
   - The next active step must be determined from the backend lifecycle/season status, not simply assumed from a client-side stage counter. If another step is legitimately next, show that step.
   - Show a clear entry point to **Promotion & relegation** when it is the next pending event, with the existing ability to inspect/run it on Live one event at a time.
   - Keep postseason movement, qualifiers, feeder rebalancing, Cup selections, Cup events and start-of-next-season guards unchanged. Fast-forwarding league play does not itself run any of them.
   - Refresh, reload, returning from Live, and completion of the final stage through manual play must settle on a correct, non-flickering flow state too. Handle an actual status fetch failure with the application's existing error/retry approach rather than claiming the postseason is finished.

4. **Keep the UI small and consistent.**
   - Remove obsolete copy, handlers and status branches only if they solely supported this intermediary component. Keep existing global navigation and ordinary season-progress information that remains useful during league play.
   - Follow the current React/TypeScript season-flow conventions and avoid unrelated UI redesign or backend rule changes.

## Architectural direction

- Inspect Dashboard season state management, its season-flow step resolver, completed-league conditional rendering, and the fast-forward completion/refresh path (including Live/navigation integration).
- Use the same authoritative lifecycle state for both selected step and rendered content. Avoid races between local stage counters and refreshed season status.
- Prefer focused frontend changes and regression tests for the transition; only touch backend code if necessary to correct an identified lifecycle/status contract issue.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Fast-forward through the final league stage: Dashboard moves to **Promotion & relegation** as the next legal step without displaying the temporary **League season complete** panel, even briefly.
- [ ] The panel's four buttons (**Inspect final tables**, **Replay history**, **Inspect Cups**, **Refresh status**) no longer appear as an intermediate completed-league action bar.
- [ ] Existing navigation to final tables, history, Cups and normal refresh remains functional elsewhere.
- [ ] Promotion/relegation is pending and playable after league completion; it has not been silently executed by fast-forward.
- [ ] The correct next step appears after a page reload, after returning from Live and after manually completing the last stage, without a misleading transient screen.
- [ ] Fast status refresh, slow status refresh and failed status refresh do not cause misleading flashes, stale step selection or false "nothing left to run" messages.
- [ ] Tests cover the end-of-league transition and the removed component; league play in progress and subsequent postseason steps remain unaffected.

## Verification

- Run the frontend test and build commands documented in `src/MtgSoloSports.Web/package.json` (or the current frontend workspace), and add focused tests around end-of-season Dashboard/season-flow transitions.
- Reproduce in a save with a full league season: fast-forward the last stages, observe the end-of-league transition, enter Promotion & relegation, and confirm that no postseason event ran automatically.
- Repeat with completion of the last stage through Live/manual play and after a page reload. Use a delayed season-status response in a test to verify that the obsolete screen never flashes.
- Validate existing final-table, history, Cups and refresh navigation.

## Out of scope

- No simulation/scoring changes and no changes to league results, promotion/relegation rules, qualifier formats, rebalancing or Cup rules.
- Do not automatically run or skip any postseason action.
- Do not remove final tables, replay/history, Cups or refresh features from their normal pages.
- No broad Dashboard card sizing, CSS overhaul or copy audit; those are covered by MSS-073 and MSS-072.
