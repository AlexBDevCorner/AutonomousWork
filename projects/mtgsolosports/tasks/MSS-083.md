# MSS-083 — Replace Career Leaders title counts with a weighted career performance ranking and season-range filters

## Goal

Make **Hall of Fame > Career Leaders** reflect the quality of an athlete's complete sporting career rather than merely counting championships. Rank athletes using accumulated, transparent **career points** earned from every official completed placement, with substantially greater weight for Superleague and major cups than lower feeder levels. Allow users to see all-time rankings or rankings calculated only from any selected inclusive range of seasons.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`; target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, default branch `main`.
- Requested 2026-10-21. In the current Hall of Fame screenshot, **Phyrexian Marauder** is ranked #1 with 4 titles (four Feeder 1 championships), while **World Breaker** is ranked #4 with 3 titles (three consecutive Superleague championships). This makes the list look like a raw title-count ranking rather than a meaningful assessment of career success.
- Phyrexian Marauder's Athlete History shows 13 seasons spanning Superleague and Feeder 1, including placements below the podium. Those seasons are achievements too; a P28 result must have a measurable, if small, ranking contribution.
- The present table shows Rank, Card, Titles and Bonus. **Active bonus is a gameplay attribute, not a measure of career performance; do not use it in this new score.**
- Universes can have a Superleague, Feeder 1/2/3, Color Cup individual and team competitions, and Type Cup (including multi-group qualification and a team Final). Historical data, old save formats and actual eligible competitions vary by universe. Inspect the existing persisted results and score/award projections before implementation.

## Requirements

1. **Introduce a career-performance score, not a renamed title count.**
   - Each athlete earns points from **every officially finalized league-season placement**, from P1 through the last finishing position. Even a low finish, e.g. P28, contributes a positive amount if the athlete has a valid completed placement.
   - A better finish in the same competition must always yield more points. Normalize by field size so P5/16 and P5/64 are not treated as identical achievements. The last place earns a small, nonzero floor, never as much as first place. Handle one-athlete and incomplete/unknown-field cases deterministically.
   - Apply **explicit competition-tier weights**. The priority order must be Superleague above Feeder 1, above Feeder 2, above Feeder 3. A Superleague title is substantially more valuable than a Feeder 1 title: with otherwise identical careers, **three Superleague championships must comfortably outscore four Feeder 1 championships**.
   - Reward first place and preferably podiums beyond their ordinary normalized placement points, while ensuring strong non-winning seasons accumulate meaningful credit. A career with broad high-level success must be able to beat one with only lower-tier wins.
   - Do not derive career points from currently accumulated athlete bonus, current league, current form/prestige, card power/toughness, or raw titles alone. Keep the existing game bonus calculation unchanged.

2. **Include officially finished major-cup performances appropriately.**
   - Award career points for **Color Cup individual results**, **Color Cup team results** and **Type Cup team results**, including valid non-podium final placements. Major tournaments must matter meaningfully and not be treated as equal to minor feeder events.
   - Differentiate **individual achievements** from **team participation**: award individual results at full relevant weight; credit team finishes to actual historically participating athletes using a documented, lower per-athlete share/weight so merely being on a strong team does not dominate individual sporting achievement.
   - Use the official athlete/result/roster association **at the time of the competition**, not an athlete's current team, league or color. Do not credit absent athletes.
   - Count each distinct official result once. In particular, do not double-count a medal and its underlying placement, duplicate a Cup via both honors and result tables, or treat qualification group and Final finishes as independent full-valued Type Cup titles. For a finalist, use the authoritative final result; for an eliminated qualifier, give a smaller, consistently documented credit for the finalized qualification result if reliable placement data is available, rather than silently fabricating a final placement.
   - Respect completed and not-yet-played cups, changed formats, multiple qualification groups, different team sizes, and universes where a cup or league tier does not exist.

3. **Define, document and test the scoring rules centrally.**
   - Implement one deterministic backend/domain/read-model score calculator or service with a versioned, named weight configuration. Avoid duplicating business formulas in React or across Hall of Fame endpoints.
   - Choose and document concrete initial weights and placement curve. A suitable *starting point to calibrate*, not a mandatory formula: `points = competitionWeight × placementFactor × achievementMultiplier × participationFactor`, with `placementFactor` monotonically descending from 1.0 at first to a small positive value at last, major-cup and league-tier weights separated explicitly, a modest title/podium premium, and team participation factored below equivalent individual results. Ensure score differences are defensible, not arbitrary to a named athlete.
   - Before settling the constants, compare a representative completed universe and explain in code docs/tests why the model rewards elite leagues and majors fairly; specifically review the Phyrexian Marauder / World Breaker example. **Do not hard-code ranks or athlete names into production scoring.**
   - Use a stable, documented precision/rounding strategy, deterministic tie-breaks (e.g. score, major-event wins, high-tier results, then athlete ID) and avoid dependence on database enumeration order.
   - Career score and breakdown should be reproducible from stored completed results for historical saves. Do not mutate historical placements or add score to the simulation RNG path.

4. **Support selectable history windows.**
   - On Hall of Fame > Career Leaders provide **All time** (default), **single season**, and **custom inclusive From season / To season** controls using available saved season numbers.
   - A period selection must **recalculate each athlete's points only from finalized results attributable to seasons in that range**, then re-sort and re-rank the entire list. Do not merely filter an already-computed all-time leaderboard or restrict to athletes still active in the last selected season.
   - Preserve historical affiliation/tier from each result's season even if an athlete has been promoted, relegated or changed teams later.
   - Validate `from <= to`; handle one season, no history, an empty selected range, missing optional cup history, and inactive/retired athletes sensibly. The season associated with each cup result must be unambiguous.
   - Keep the selected period when navigating back from an athlete profile if practical using existing URL/state conventions. Changing universe must reset or reconcile invalid filters.

5. **Present the ranking so its meaning is clear.**
   - Change the primary ordering/value to **Career points** (or `Performance points`) and show the selected period in the heading. Retain **Titles** as an optional supporting statistic, but do not sort by raw title count.
   - Make the score auditable: provide an accessible compact breakdown (e.g. league tiers, individual cups, team cups) for an athlete, ideally with per-season and/or per-event contributions scoped to the selected range. Display the period's titles as supporting statistics if shown.
   - Preserve card navigation and compact dark-theme style. Ensure responsive rendering on desktop and mobile with useful empty/loading/error states. Do not misleadingly keep the **Bonus** column as though it were part of the score; remove or clearly separate it as an unrelated metric.

6. **Keep the system backward-compatible and efficient.**
   - Read from authoritative completed historical results and existing persistence wherever possible, including old saves and universes without three feeder tiers.
   - Do not award provisional in-progress standings, incomplete Type Cup stages, or future/unplayed season results. Results from already finalized competitions may be counted according to their persisted official status even when other competitions in that season are unfinished.
   - Keep calculations read-only and avoid N+1 queries or repeated re-aggregation per athlete. Support many seasons, athletes and user-selected ranges without expensive client-side transfer of the entire history.
   - Reuse the scoring/read model for both all-time and period-specific queries to prevent inconsistencies.

## Architectural direction

- Trace existing Hall of Fame Career Leaders API/handler/projection and UI, completed league standings, athlete season-by-season history, and Color/Type Cup persisted results. Identify the **authoritative single source** for a league finish and each distinct Cup finish/roster appearance.
- Put ranking aggregation in the backend. Return stable score, tie-break fields, period metadata and optional breakdown in compatible API DTOs. Reuse the existing frontend data fetching and history/navigation conventions.
- Ensure qualification stages and the Type Cup Final have unambiguous stage identity (particularly multi-group Type Cups); deduplicate results with persistent event/season/athlete keys, not names.
- Prefer a focused enhancement of Career Leaders over a global replacement of unrelated athlete statistics or Hall of Fame categories.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Career Leaders sorts by documented **career performance points**, not number of titles or active bonus.
- [ ] Every eligible finalized finish, including a last-place or P28 finish, contributes a positive, deterministic placement score; higher placements in an otherwise identical event score more.
- [ ] Superleague outranks feeder tiers by substantial weights. Three Superleague titles beat four Feeder 1 titles in a controlled comparison with otherwise equal histories.
- [ ] Color Cup individual/team and Type Cup results are represented with appropriate major-event and individual-vs-team weights, with no duplicated honors, medals, rounds or qualification/Final points.
- [ ] All time, one season and arbitrary valid inclusive season ranges produce independently recomputed rankings and per-period supporting values.
- [ ] Filters include historical/retired athletes; season-specific league tiers/teams are preserved after promotions and other later changes.
- [ ] An in-progress league season / unfinished Cup does not create provisional credit, but already completed official competitions remain correctly handled.
- [ ] The UI explains score and period and allows the user to inspect where an athlete's points came from; layout remains readable on narrow widths.
- [ ] Old saves, missing optional competitions, one-athlete events, tied scores and empty ranges are handled without crashes, nondeterministic ranking or bogus points.
- [ ] Focused backend and frontend regression tests pass; existing competition mechanics, persistent history, athlete bonuses and unrelated Hall of Fame views are unchanged.

## Verification

1. Add unit tests for the scoring curve and league-tier weights: P1/P2/P28/last, different field sizes, one-member field, 3×Superleague title vs 4×Feeder 1 titles, strong non-winner vs lower-tier title accumulation, and deterministic ties.
2. Add integration/projection tests with at least two completed seasons, different feeder-tier assignments, retired/inactive athletes, major cups, a Color Cup with separate individual/team credit, and a multi-group Type Cup with qualifiers and finalists; verify every event is counted exactly once.
3. Test all-time vs one-season vs arbitrary inclusive range and boundary validation, with explicit expected score sums and reordered positions. Ensure a newly incomplete season does not contaminate earlier-period scores.
4. Compare the displayed Phyrexian Marauder / World Breaker example against the actual scoring components of a representative universe, and report any surprising ordering with its breakdown before finalizing coefficients. Do not artificially force a particular rank without general scoring rationale.
5. Add/update frontend tests for period selector, sorting, score breakdown, back navigation, changing universe and empty state. Check desktop and narrow/mobile views.
6. Run relevant repository .NET tests plus frontend tests, typecheck and build, using the commands/scripts current in `MtgSoloSports`; include the scoring rules and before/after ranking examples in the PR description.

## Out of scope

- No changes to league/cup fixtures, simulations, promotion or relegation rules, qualification, team selection, RNG, bonuses, or awarded titles/medals.
- No data rewriting, migration of historical sporting results, or retroactive changes to recorded finishes.
- No global redesign of the athlete profile, Hall of Fame sections other than Career Leaders, or unrelated dashboards.
- No externally published cross-universe leaderboard; ranking is per selected universe.
