# MSS-076 — Turn Athlete Profile into a clear at-a-glance career summary

## Goal

Make the **Athlete Profile** pane on the Athlete page a visually clear, useful, at-a-glance summary of the athlete's identity and entire sporting career. Important attributes and aggregate achievements must be immediately readable, without opening other panes. Preserve the existing detailed panes for deeper breakdowns of those same characteristics. **Only the Athlete Profile pane is in scope.**

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`; target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, branch `main`.
- User report on 2026-10-11 with screenshot of **Expendable Troops**. The current top Athlete Profile area shows a card image, athlete name, `Creature — Human Soldier`, and small, low-contrast lines such as `White · {1}{W} · Human, Soldier · ulg`, `Active · Superleague`, and `Type Cup nationality: Soldier`.
- Color and current league are already present but are too visually subdued. Mana cost is irrelevant to this sports game. If `ulg` refers to the original MTG expansion/set, that metadata is irrelevant here too.
- The user wants one prominent, concise identity-and-career overview, while other panels retain detailed History, Honours, Cups, Bonus, Selections, etc.
- MSS-073 separately addresses panel sizing/responsive grid and the Athlete identity/header's excessive empty space. MSS-072 separately addresses wording/capitalization across the UI. Coordinate with their existing work, but do not expand either task's scope or modify other panes.

## Requirements

1. **Keep relevant identity information, and remove irrelevant card metadata.**
   - Retain athlete name and card art, and show **Color** prominently and legibly (e.g. `White`). Do not bury it in tiny, faint, semi-transparent text.
   - Retain the meaningful creature type/subtypes (e.g. `Human Soldier`), **Type Cup nationality** (e.g. `Soldier`), and **current league** (e.g. `Superleague`), with easily recognizable labels.
   - Do not display MTG **mana cost** in this pane. Remove the MTG **set code/name** (such as `ulg`) if that field is indeed the expansion identifier; verify its source before removal.
   - Keep current athlete status (active / pool / other states) where meaningful, using a clear human-readable label. Render absent values honestly, rather than inventing a league or nationality.

2. **Add career season totals by tier, with numbers for every level.**
   - Show numeric totals with explicit labels for **Superleague seasons**, **Feeder 1 seasons**, **Feeder 2 seasons**, **Feeder 3 seasons**, and **Pool seasons**.
   - Display `0` for valid zero totals; handle universes with fewer feeder tiers gracefully (do not suggest nonexistent competitions occurred).
   - Derive counts from actual athlete history/season participation according to the game's existing domain rules; count seasons, not stages or rounds, and avoid accidental duplicate counting. Investigate the established rules for promotions, mid-season moves and pool years, and keep them consistent with detailed history.

3. **Add concise aggregate career achievements.**
   - Numeric **Superleague titles**, **Feeder 1 titles**, **Feeder 2 titles**, and **Feeder 3 titles**, clearly distinguished from season appearances and promotion/relegation.
   - Numeric **Color caps** and **Type caps**, using the game's existing definitions of Cup selection/appearances (verify current domain semantics and avoid counting an individual Cup multiple times).
   - Aggregated **Cup medals**, making **gold / silver / bronze** counts easy to distinguish; include medals earned across applicable Cup types and use persisted results/honours as the source of truth. Do not count a single honour twice or conflate Cup medals with league titles.
   - **Current active bonus**, shown prominently with its proper value, sign/unit and any brief necessary explanatory context. Use the bonus that actually applies now, not historical, expired, or base-only figures; show a clear `None` / `0` state when applicable.

4. **Make every field visibly useful from a UI/UX perspective.**
   - This is **not** a task to append a long list of small gray metadata. Use a compact, thoughtful layout with a clear hierarchy: identity (card/name/color/type/current league), career participation by tier, championships, Cup representation/medals, and active bonus.
   - Each requested property must be **clearly visible, labeled, scannable, and readable**, including zero values. Use sufficient text size and contrast on the existing dark theme; do not rely on hover, tooltip, color alone, or expansion to discover essential values.
   - Prefer aligned short labels and prominent numeric values (such as grouped metric cells) rather than sentences or run-on strings. Make related data easy to compare, especially the four league tiers and their title counts.
   - Avoid an enormous header or excessive whitespace. Respect the current Athlete page responsive layout and MSS-073's bounded/card sizing work; prevent clipping, overlap, horizontal scrolling and unreadably compressed numbers on common desktop/laptop and narrower widths. If necessary, reflow sections within **this pane** at smaller widths rather than hiding fields.
   - Preserve the MTG artwork's aspect ratio, athlete name, current navigation actions and any existing necessary controls.

5. **Use authoritative sporting data and keep results current.**
   - Identify whether existing Athlete API/view-model already exposes each aggregate; reuse the same persisted data/definitions as the detailed panes wherever practical.
   - Add a focused aggregate/projection or endpoint fields only where needed for this pane; do not modify gameplay calculations, mutate history or duplicate achievement logic with fragile UI parsing.
   - Data must update after seasons, league titles, Cup selections/results, and bonus changes; it must remain correct across navigation and refresh.
   - Handle new athletes, unselected athletes, athletes with no medals/titles, pool athletes, and saves with different numbers of feeder levels. Clearly distinguish zero from genuinely unavailable data.

## Architectural direction

- Inspect the Athlete page/header component, its current data hooks/API contract, and existing History, Honours, Selections, Cups and Bonus read models for definitions and reusable aggregated values.
- Implement the summary as a focused change to the existing **Athlete Profile** pane/header. A small reusable metric display inside this pane is fine; do not redesign the whole Athlete page.
- Reuse established styling, responsive breakpoints, and competition terminology; keep data derivation centralized and testable rather than aggregating UI strings.
- Existing detailed panes must remain visually and functionally unchanged. Avoid conflicts with MSS-072/MSS-073.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Profile visibly shows athlete image/name, prominent **Color**, creature types (e.g. Human Soldier), **Type Cup nationality**, **current league** and meaningful current status.
- [ ] Mana cost is absent; MTG set code/name is absent when `ulg` or equivalent is confirmed to be set metadata.
- [ ] Five distinct and clearly labeled numeric season totals are visible: **Superleague / Feeder 1 / Feeder 2 / Feeder 3 / Pool**.
- [ ] Four distinct, clearly labeled numeric league title totals are visible: **Superleague / Feeder 1 / Feeder 2 / Feeder 3**.
- [ ] **Color caps**, **Type caps**, **Cup gold/silver/bronze medals**, and **current active bonus** are visibly and correctly summarized.
- [ ] All important values are readable at a glance in the dark theme: meaningful font size, sufficient contrast, clear label/value hierarchy and grouping; no low-opacity miniature data dump and no reliance on hover/expand to find required data.
- [ ] Layout remains clean with card artwork and controls intact at desktop and narrow widths, without excessive blank space, overlap, clipped fields or page-level horizontal overflow.
- [ ] Aggregates match actual persisted sporting history and their detailed counterparts; verified with test cases for new/no-achievement athletes, decorated veterans, pool athletes, zero counts and different feeder-level configurations.
- [ ] Refresh/navigation and new season/Cup results reflect updated numbers without stale career summaries.
- [ ] **No other Athlete panes** (History, Honours, Cups, Records, Bonus, Selections, etc.) are redesigned or have their information/control sets changed. No Dashboard changes or competition-rule changes.

## Verification

1. Run the frontend's documented lint/type-check/test/build commands and any relevant backend automated tests.
2. Add or update targeted tests for aggregation/formatting, especially distinct season/title tiers, caps, medals, bonus status, zeros and missing tiers.
3. Inspect the Athlete Profile visually in dark theme at wide desktop, laptop and narrow viewport widths. Verify all requested fields remain clearly visible and the artwork/navigation remain usable.
4. Cross-check several saved athletes with different histories against the existing History/Honours/Cups/Selections/Bonus details; navigate away/back and reload to confirm persisted values.
5. Confirm other Athlete panes and their current layout/scroll behavior remain unchanged.

## Out of scope

- No redesign, removal or repurposing of any other Athlete page pane; those remain the places for deeper details.
- No Dashboard, league, Cup, qualifier, gameplay, scoring, promotion/relegation or selection-rule changes.
- No global UI wording/typography cleanup (MSS-072) or broad pane sizing/grid redesign (MSS-073).
- No speculative new career statistics beyond those explicitly requested; possible future profile additions are separate tasks.
