# MSS-048 — Add Cup history to athlete profiles

## Goal

Add a **Cup history** section to each athlete profile, following the same general presentation principle as the existing League history: show the athlete's historical Cup participation by **season, place, and result**.

The history must include both **individual Cups** and **team Cups**.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`; target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`; default branch: `main`.
- Athlete profiles already expose League history, giving a season-by-season view of the athlete's league participation/results.
- Cup participation should be discoverable from the same athlete profile rather than requiring users to navigate through historical Cup pages.
- MtgSoloSports has both individual and team Cup events. Both kinds of participation belong in an athlete's Cup history.
- Historical data should come from persisted competition results/history rather than from current league/team state.
- Follow `AGENTS.md`, `docs/game-rules.md`, the existing vertical-slice architecture, and established athlete-profile UI conventions.

## Requirements

1. Add a **Cup history** section/view to the athlete profile alongside the existing League history.
2. Use the same core information model as League history where applicable:
   - **Season**
   - **Place**
   - **Result**
3. Include every completed Cup in which the athlete participated, covering both:
   - individual Cup competitions;
   - team Cup competitions.
4. For individual Cups, show the athlete's own final placement and result.
5. For team Cups, include the athlete's participation in the team event and show the athlete's team final placement/result for that Cup. Make the event/team context clear enough that users do not interpret the placement as an individual placement.
6. Clearly identify which Cup/event each history row belongs to. If multiple Cup competitions can occur in the same season, rows must remain unambiguous.
7. Order the history consistently with League history, preferably newest season/events first unless the existing athlete-history convention dictates otherwise.
8. Use persisted historical results so old Cup participation appears automatically for existing seasons. Do not require competitions to be replayed or regenerated.
9. Do not derive team-Cup history only from the athlete's current type/team membership. An athlete's historical row must remain correct even if later seasons place that athlete on a different team or make them inactive.
10. Handle athletes with:
    - no Cup history;
    - only individual Cup history;
    - only team Cup history;
    - both individual and team Cup history;
    - multiple Cup appearances within the same season.
11. Reuse the existing League-history visual language/components where practical so League history and Cup history feel like parts of the same athlete profile rather than unrelated tables.
12. Preserve existing League history behavior and all existing athlete-profile statistics, navigation, honours, and competition results.

## Architectural direction

- Start from the existing athlete-profile League history slice and follow the same data-flow pattern rather than introducing a parallel architecture.
- Inspect the persisted Cup/result models for both individual and team events and project athlete-specific history from authoritative historical records.
- Prefer a unified athlete Cup-history DTO/view model that can represent both individual and team Cup entries while retaining enough event metadata to distinguish them in the UI.
- Do not infer historical membership from current entities when the historical competition result already records the participants/team.
- Keep sporting calculations unchanged; this task exposes historical data rather than changing Cup simulation or scoring.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Athlete profiles contain a Cup history section consistent with the existing League history experience.
- [ ] Each Cup-history entry shows at least season, place, and result.
- [ ] The Cup/event is identifiable for every row.
- [ ] Completed individual Cup participation appears in the athlete's history with the athlete's final placement/result.
- [ ] Completed team Cup participation appears in the athlete's history with the team's final placement/result and is clearly recognizable as a team event.
- [ ] An athlete who participated in both individual and team Cups in the same season sees all applicable entries.
- [ ] Multiple Cup events in one season are represented independently without overwriting or collapsing unrelated results.
- [ ] Existing historical Cup data is shown without replaying seasons.
- [ ] Historical team-Cup entries remain correct even if the athlete's later/current team or activity state differs.
- [ ] Athletes with no Cup history get an appropriate empty state rather than an error or misleading row.
- [ ] Existing League history remains unchanged and continues to work.
- [ ] Backend/frontend tests cover individual Cup history, team Cup history, multiple events in one season, and no-history cases.

## Verification

1. Run the standard backend build/test checks documented in `AGENTS.md`.
2. Run the frontend checks documented by the repository, including typecheck/tests/build where applicable.
3. Verify an athlete with a completed individual Cup appearance shows the correct season, event, place, and result.
4. Verify an athlete from a completed team Cup shows the correct team-event entry and team placement/result.
5. Verify an athlete with both types of Cup participation in one season shows both entries.
6. Verify historical entries remain correct after progressing to later seasons where the athlete's team/type participation may differ.
7. Verify an athlete with no Cup appearances has a clean empty state and existing League history still renders normally.

## Out of scope

- Changing Cup qualification, team formation, simulation, scoring, tie-breaks, or competition formats.
- Changing historical League calculations or League-history semantics.
- Reworking athlete-profile layout beyond what is needed to add Cup history consistently.
- Introducing new Cup result data solely for presentation when the authoritative historical result already exists.
