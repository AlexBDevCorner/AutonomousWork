# MSS-120 — Run a 32-to-16 Color Cup with full score carryover

## Goal

Activate the confirmed individual Color Cup format in the backend: 16 rounds with 32 athletes, cutoff to 16 finalists, then 16 more rounds with the full qualifying totals carried forward.

## Context

- Project `mtgsolosports`; target repository `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports` (`main`).
- Follow MSS-119 format versioning and historical compatibility groundwork.
- **Approved sporting format:** the initial 32 selected athletes (4 per each of 8 colors) compete in rounds 1–16. Qualification positions 17–32 are final and eliminated. Positions 1–16 advance to rounds 17–32, with 100% of their first-phase points intact. The final podium is based on total points across all 32 rounds for finalists. No score reset and no score weighting.
- Active career bonuses apply as before; the Cup awards no new bonus and no league championship points. Team Color Cup, Type Cup and league stages remain unchanged.
- Existing primitives support 16-person rounds: `RoundSimulator.SimulateWithFieldSize` and feeder-qualifier helpers. The existing individual Cup path assumes 32 participants in every one of 16 rounds and therefore needs explicit two-phase orchestration.

## Requirements

1. When starting a **new** individual Color Cup, bind it to the new format version provided by MSS-119; existing started/completed legacy events retain their 16-round format.
2. Play rounds 1–16 with the original 32-person roster and current scoring rules.
3. At the end of round 16, deterministically classify athletes (respect the established score/placement/base/tie-break ordering) and freeze qualifiers 1–16 versus eliminated places 17–32. Persist or reproducibly derive the exact cutoff and any seeded tie-breaking RNG advancement so that both full-run and round-by-round execution match.
4. Play rounds 17–32 with only those 16 qualifiers. Each finalist's round-17 cumulative score equals their round-16 score; every new result adds to this cumulative total. Use existing 1–16 scoring table positions. Do not reset or reduce accumulated points.
5. Resolve the final results: classify finalists 1–16 by combined totals; classify eliminated 17–32 by qualifying rank. Preserve historical results, medal and championship honours for the final podium.
6. Persist complete immutable per-round payloads, accurate position/placement counts (32-person first phase, 16-person final), correct final standings for all 32 entrants, deterministic RNG chain and replayable history. Never fabricate round appearances for eliminated athletes.
7. Both step-by-step and one-shot execution, including resumption immediately before/after the cutoff, must converge to the same standings, checksum and RNG. Support the legacy 16-round run/read path for older events.
8. Maintain no league standing/round changes, no career bonus earning and unchanged team Cup outcomes.

## Architectural direction

- Direct Endpoint -> Handler; no MediatR.
- Use a Cup-specific two-phase orchestrator, not a mixed-size `StageCalculator.Accumulate` call. Reuse field-size simulation primitives without affecting normal league rounds.
- Preserve tie-breaking and numerical fixed-point determinism; be explicit about phase boundary RNG draws and score continuity.
- Keep detailed UI/report redesign for MSS-121. Any API contract addition needed to expose completed results may be done here, but avoid unrelated frontend refactoring.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A new Cup produces precisely sixteen 32-person rounds followed by sixteen 16-person rounds.
- [ ] Exactly 16 qualify; qualifying ranks 17–32 are fixed; each finalist's carried score matches round 16.
- [ ] Final ranks 1–16 reflect accumulated first- and second-phase points, and all 32 athletes appear in final standings.
- [ ] One-shot and stepwise paths, including restart/resume at round 16/17, are reproducible and byte-consistent where applicable.
- [ ] Existing historical 16-round events continue to resolve and replay accurately.
- [ ] Medals, honours, RNG, audit data, no-bonus and no-league-points guarantees remain valid.
- [ ] Full repository verification succeeds.

## Verification

Add focused fixture-based and property/edge-case tests covering cutoff ties, RNG checkpoint, carryover, 16-person scoring, final ranks, legacy events, partial state resume and full-vs-step convergence. Run target `AGENTS.md` .NET and frontend required checks.

## Out of scope

- New presentation, history page highlights or bulk report changes (MSS-121).
- League stages, team Color Cup, Type Cup, Chaos Cup or selection weights.
- Score reset, partial carryover, score weighting or new score tables.
- Rewriting or re-simulating any historical Cup.
