# MSS-137 - Show upcoming Chaos Cup steps during league play

## Goal

Show eligible players where Chaos Cup fits in the season from the first league stage. The Dashboard should show it as upcoming until league play ends.

## Context

- Project `mtgsolosports`, target `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports` on `main`.
- Reproduced in an existing save at Season 19, Stage 1 of 32. The save's persisted Chaos Cup activation starts in Season 19, but the Dashboard's **Next step** timeline jumps from **League play** to **Promotion & relegation**.
- `SeasonLifecycleEvaluator.EvaluateInProgressAsync` returns the default `ChaosEligible` value during league play. `seasonFlowSteps.ts` uses `chaosEligible` or a current Chaos action to decide whether to include the two steps.

## Requirements

1. During league play, show **Chaos Cup field** and **Chaos Cup** as upcoming steps immediately after **League play** and before promotion or inaugural movement, whenever the current season is eligible.
2. Use the save's persisted Chaos activation and the current season to determine eligibility. Keep both steps hidden in seasons earlier than the activation season.
3. Keep **League play** current and the Chaos steps inactive until league play is complete. After the final league stage, the normal Dashboard flow offers **Select Chaos Cup field**, then the existing Chaos Cup controls after field selection.

## Architectural direction

- Reuse `ChaosCupLifecycle.IsEligible` and the existing `ChaosEligible` season-status field. Start with `src/MtgSoloSports/Features/Seasons/SeasonLifecycle/SeasonLifecycleEvaluator.cs` and `src/MtgSoloSports.Web/src/features/dashboard/seasonFlowSteps.ts`.
- Keep eligibility separate from `legalNextActions`. Reuse the existing Dashboard step styling and action gating.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Opening or refreshing the Dashboard at Stage 1 in an eligible season shows both Chaos steps in order, with league play still active. They remain upcoming during intermediate stages.
- [ ] A legacy save before its activation season shows the original timeline. In that same save's first eligible season, both steps appear from Stage 1.
- [ ] League play remains the only legal next action until Stage 32 completes. The postseason then offers field selection, followed by the Chaos Cup, before movement.
- [ ] Focused season-status and Dashboard tests cover eligible and ineligible league play, and verify that early visibility does not unlock any Chaos action.

## Verification

- Run `dotnet test tests/MtgSoloSports.Tests --configuration Release --filter FullyQualifiedName~ChaosLifecycleTests` and the repository-required backend checks.
- Run `npm test --prefix src/MtgSoloSports.Web`, `npm run typecheck --prefix src/MtgSoloSports.Web`, and `npm run build --prefix src/MtgSoloSports.Web` from the target repository root.
- Verify the Dashboard at Stage 1 and after the final league stage using test saves.

## Out of scope

- Changes to Chaos Cup activation boundaries, save schema, tournament rules, simulation, or postseason action order.
- Changes to the Cups hub, Live controls, or unrelated Dashboard layout.
