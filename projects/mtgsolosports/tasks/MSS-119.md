# MSS-119 — Give individual Color Cup a 32-to-16 knockout midpoint

## Goal

Differentiate the individual Color Cup from an ordinary league stage through a two-phase competition: the 32 selected athletes compete over 16 rounds, then the top 16 contest a further 16-round final. **Draft for product discussion only; not authorized for implementation.**

## Context

- Project `mtgsolosports` -> `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports` (`main`); no MediatR, direct Endpoint -> Handler.
- User initially proposed 32 total rounds; after discussion expressed interest in eliminating half the athletes after the first 16. A purely presentational 16+16 split without elimination has been rejected as redundant.
- Current Color Cup is a single 16-round competition with 32 athletes from 8 colors; active bonus applies, but no league championship points or newly earned career bonus.
- Current constraints: `RulesV1.ColorCupIndividualRounds == RoundsPerStage` (16); `RunColorCupIndividualHandler.PlayRound` runs the full 32-member roster; `StageCalculator.Accumulate` expects all 32 in each round; `ColorCupIndividualInvariants` checks 32 placements in every round and exactly 16 appearances for every athlete.
- Useful existing primitives: `RoundSimulator.SimulateWithFieldSize(..., 16)` and `StageCalculator.AccumulateWithFieldSize(..., 16, 16)` for feeder qualifiers, plus `RankWithFieldSize`. They can be reused by an independent two-phase Color Cup orchestration rather than redesigning the simulation engine.

## Proposed requirements (subject to product decision)

1. Qualifying phase: rounds 1–16 with all 32 athletes. Rank qualifying scores deterministically under existing tie-break rules; only the top 16 advance.
2. Final phase: rounds 17–32 among the 16 qualified athletes, using the existing position 1–16 points scale and active bonuses. **All points earned in rounds 1–16 carry over in full.** Final-phase points are added to the qualifying totals, with no reset, reduction or weighting.
3. Final Cup placements 1–16 must be determined by the final-phase results; overall placements 17–32 must reflect the qualifying phase. Eliminated athletes remain in the Cup standings and history but do not play final rounds.
4. Preserve round-by-round and one-shot simulation, deterministic RNG chains, integrity checks, and persisted payload replay across both phases; the round-17 roster must be derived deterministically from completed qualifying results, even when the user resumes a partially played Cup. Ensure carried-forward cumulative totals are preserved exactly at the cutoff.
5. Keep active bonus application as before while awarding no league championship points and no new career bonus. Preserve the distinct individual Color Cup podium and honours.
6. Preserve full backward compatibility with completed historical 16-round Cups, without re-simulation, mutation or reinterpretation. The effective Cup format must be derivable reliably from persisted competition metadata, version or history.
7. Update progress/read APIs, per-round placement validation (32 before cutoff, 16 after), final ranking persistence, history/replay, downloadable reports and any relevant UI so that the cutoff is clear.
8. Keep implementation in small reviewable PRs, for example rules/orchestration, persistence/history/backward compatibility, and UI/report updates with focused tests.

## Open product decisions

- **Decided:** scores carry over in full between phases. This is deliberately different from Type Cup, where phases reset scores. Do not reopen the reset/partial carryover question in implementation.
- Is the final classification of places 17–32 based on the precise qualifying rank (recommended)?
- Should the new format apply to new Cup editions only (recommended), with started/finished old editions retaining their original format?
- Optional UX enhancement: a visible top-16 qualification cutoff after round 16 and a 'Qualified / Eliminated' view; avoid scope creep into unrelated visualization.

## Architectural direction

- Reuse `RoundSimulator.SimulateWithFieldSize` / `StageCalculator.AccumulateWithFieldSize` for the 16-athlete final; keep the shared league `RoundsPerStage` unchanged.
- Build a Color Cup-specific two-phase orchestrator for qualification, finalist selection, full score carryover and aggregation. Do not force a mixed 32/16 roster into a single `StageCalculator.Accumulate` call. Preserve original 32-position first-phase placement counts and valid 16-position second-phase counts in the final tie-breaking logic.
- Add format-version-aware validation of 16-round historical events and new 32-round phased events.
- Prefer several small PRs to a monolithic change. New features beyond this tournament must not be bundled.

## Acceptance criteria (provisional)

- [ ] A new event runs 16 qualifying rounds (32 athletes) and 16 final rounds (16 athletes), with deterministic selection at the cutoff.
- [ ] The complete first-phase score carries over into the final, and round 17 starts from each finalist's round-16 cumulative total. This is deterministic, documented and tested.
- [ ] All 32 athletes have final classified placements: finalists 1–16, eliminated 17–32.
- [ ] Historical 16-round Cup events are still readable, replayable and exportable.
- [ ] Round-by-round and one-shot execution produce equivalent final results; resume after cutoff is correct.
- [ ] Existing league points, active bonus earning, team Color Cup rules and unrelated tournaments are unchanged.
- [ ] Cup UI/API/report and focused tests reflect the new format without breaking old saves.

## Verification

Targeted simulator, Color Cup step/full run, round replay, history, export and compatibility tests; then all required backend/frontend verification in target `AGENTS.md`. Exercise an existing 16-round save and a new 32-round phased Cup.

## Out of scope

- No change to league stages, team Color Cup, Type Cup or Chaos Cup.
- No new scoring table, score reset, partial carryover, weighting or broad balance changes.
- No retroactive recalculation of historic Color Cup results.
- No approval to implement until the product decisions above are resolved and task is explicitly set to `ready`.
