# MSS-119 — Version individual Color Cup formats without breaking historical editions

## Goal

Lay the small, backward-compatible rules/persistence foundation for the confirmed two-phase individual Color Cup format, while leaving existing Cup runtime behavior unchanged until MSS-120.

## Context

- Project `mtgsolosports`; target `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports` (`main`); direct vertical-slice Endpoint -> Handler, no MediatR.
- **Sporting decisions confirmed by user**: 32 athletes compete in 16 qualifying rounds; the top 16 advance; qualifying places 17–32 become their final tournament places. The 16 finalists play rounds 17–32, keeping **100% of points from qualification**, and the overall total decides places 1–16 and podium. No reset, no partial carryover or weighting. This is intentionally distinct from Type Cup.
- Apply the new format only to **new individual Cup editions**. Historical 16-round editions, including those already started before the new format is enabled, keep their original rules and results.
- Current `RulesV1.ColorCupIndividualRounds` defaults to 16 and validation enforces equality with `RoundsPerStage`; `ColorCupIndividualInvariants` currently expects all 32 participants in all 16 rounds.
- This is the first of three **small sequential tasks**: MSS-119 (format/compatibility groundwork), MSS-120 (simulation and persistence behavior), MSS-121 (UI, replay presentation and reports).

## Requirements

1. Introduce a stable edition-specific individual Color Cup format/version discriminator: legacy 16 rounds of 32 athletes versus the new 16 rounds of 32 athletes followed by 16 rounds of 16 finalists. Use persisted metadata, versioned round records or an equally robust save-scoped approach; do not infer historical format from today's default configuration.
2. Existing completed and partially started 16-round Cups must be recognized as legacy without manual save edits. New format must be selected and fixed **at the start of a new Cup**, not silently during a partial run.
3. Decouple Color Cup format/rule validation from the global `RoundsPerStage == 16` requirement, without changing the 16-round league stage count.
4. Create format-aware rules/validation primitives for legal phase and roster sizes (rounds 1–16 have 32 placements; rounds 17–32 have 16, in the new format), stage completion and per-athlete participation counts (32 for finalists, 16 for eliminated), while keeping legacy validation behavior unchanged.
5. Ensure future MSS-120 can preserve full cumulative qualifying scores at the cutoff and classify eliminated 17–32 by round-16 rank; do not activate the new sporting behavior in this preparatory task.
6. Add focused tests for old persisted records, already-started Cups and format boundary constraints.

## Architectural direction

- Keep the default simulation flow legacy in this preparatory PR; switch newly-started Cups to the new format only in MSS-120 when its full orchestration exists.
- Prefer a small additive schema/metadata change and narrowly scoped validators to a global `RulesV1` redesign. Do not change standard league, Type Cup, Chaos Cup or team Color Cup rules.
- Persist enough format information for both historical reads and interrupted/resumed Cup runs. Avoid reopening approved sporting decisions.
- Keep PR reviewable and exclude MSS-120/MSS-121 implementation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each completed or in-progress legacy Cup is unambiguously recognized as 16×32 and remains readable, replayable and unchanged.
- [ ] A future newly started phased Cup can persist/reload a distinct 16×32 + 16×16 format without relying on runtime default rules.
- [ ] Format-aware validation supports both event shapes; old round payloads and results still pass.
- [ ] Standard leagues still play 16 rounds; no changes to bonus earning, league points or other Cups.
- [ ] Existing and focused compatibility tests pass; full repository verification succeeds.

## Verification

Run focused Color Cup persistence/rules/invariant tests, including legacy save fixtures, then required backend/frontend commands documented in target `AGENTS.md`.

## Out of scope

- Actually running or resolving the 32-to-16 format (MSS-120).
- Frontend display, live cutoff indicators or report/export enhancements (MSS-121).
- Changing historical sporting results, scoring tables, bonus mechanics or Type Cup.
- Any score reset, reduced carryover, weighting or extra eliminations.
