# MSS-142 - Highlight mathematically clinched league titles and postseason outcomes before season end

## Goal

As a season progresses, clearly distinguish an athlete's **mathematically guaranteed** championship/placement-zone outcome from the merely provisional zone shown in league standings. Show early locked titles, direct promotions, qualifier places, retention and relegations only when remaining official stages cannot change the relevant result.

## Context

- Project `mtgsolosports`; target `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`, branch `main`.
- MSS-141 adds rules-aware league table zones and a compact legend for Superleague, F1/F2/F3, Season 1 inaugural and supported legacy saves. **Build on MSS-141 instead of adding a second set of rank cutoffs.**
- The canonical season placement uses **championship points awarded for completed stage rankings** (standard scoring table: 77 for first down to 1 for 32nd in current rules), not cumulative raw round/stage score, active bonus, Career Leaders points or Chaos/Cup scores. Each league season currently has 32 stages; read the actual save-owned rules and completed-stage state.
- Current league rank is provisional until the league's final official stage is complete. A stage with only some rounds played does not yet award final championship points and must be treated as not completed.
- An athlete may be guaranteed **a rank band** without a fixed exact rank, or may be guaranteed #1/champion outright. Qualification participation is not promotion: a guaranteed qualifier berth does not guarantee its eventual outcome. Structural roster rebalancing is distinct from competitive relegation/promotion.

## Requirements

1. Calculate a read-only, per-athlete **proven guarantee** from official completed stage results, current championship-point totals, saved season scoring rules, remaining stage count, and official tie-breaking uncertainty. Do not simulate future results, consume sporting RNG, mutate/save speculative data, or use probability/expected scores as a substitute for proof.
2. Use safe mathematical bounds for remaining championship points. For each athlete, establish a possible minimum and maximum final official points based on the remaining **unawarded** stages and the save's valid stage-placement points. Conservatively bound possible finishing ranks by comparing this athlete's interval with every other athlete's interval. A tied-point boundary must be treated as unresolved unless the existing official tie-break rules prove an invariant outcome for **all** remaining possibilities. Conservative false negatives are acceptable; **false guarantees are not**. A simple safe proof may use strict score inequalities and pessimistic treatment of all possible ties. Avoid costly combinatorial enumeration.
3. Distinguish at least these guarantees, where defined by the MSS-141 season/league rule bands:
   - **Champion locked:** mathematical final rank #1 in any league before its final stage.
   - **Direct promotion locked:** all possible finishing ranks fall in that season's direct-promotion band (e.g. F1 champion, F2/F3 1–8, Season 1 F1 1–4).
   - **Qualifier berth locked:** all possible ranks fall within the *same* exact qualification band (challenger or defending incumbent, with target boundary); a possible #1/direct-promotion result must **not** be mislabeled as a guaranteed qualifier appearance.
   - **Safety/retention locked:** all possible finishing ranks stay in the relevant retention band.
   - **Relegation locked:** all possible ranks fall in the automatic-relegation band (Superleague/F1/F2); F3 must never be labeled relegated to Pool under the normal competitive rules.
   If the mathematically possible range spans different bands, do not claim any one exact zone is secured. A clinched championship may coexist with a separately guaranteed direct movement; display them without redundant visual noise.
4. Use authoritative **league + season rules** for thresholds, including the inaugural Season 1 behavior and any supported legacy/single-tier save. A locked label must never promise a qualifier or movement that does not exist in that edition. Where rules or complete official point data cannot be trusted, fail safely with no guarantee rather than invent one.
5. Recompute/read guarantees for the requested save, season and league as official stages finish, across reload and season switches. Never reuse state from another league or season; do not infer a lock from stale UI sorts, stage matrix ranks or incomplete stage/round data.
6. Extend the MSS-141 season standings design with a **distinct, compact confirmed status** (e.g. icon + “Clinched”/“Guaranteed” and specific outcome), separate from the current projected zone color. Provide accessible text or tooltip explaining what was clinched. Do not use color as the only signal; maintain a readable 32-row table with no giant status column or mobile overflow.
7. Once the league season is officially complete, show final zones/final title under MSS-141 but do not describe them as *early clinches*. Historical seasons must never acquire bogus “clinched early” badges just because final results are known. Do not confuse a qualifier berth with a finished qualifier victory or next-season roster outcome.
8. Keep the guarantee calculation deterministic and cheap for repeated standings reads (32 athletes and a small number of stages); prefer pure, tested domain calculation with additive read-only projections, not stored mutable flags or a new background job. Avoid changing the existing simulation/tie-break logic.

## Architectural direction

- A small pure classifier is appropriate: per-athlete final-score interval and pessimistic rank interval (strict comparisons; treat unresolved equality as potentially adverse), mapped to MSS-141's rule-aware bands. Use integer/fixed-point arithmetic throughout and check the saved scoring config rather than hardcoding 77/1/32 for all editions.
- Reuse backend authoritative completed-stage counts and championship totals if readily available. If frontend already has sufficient trustworthy fields, a pure frontend calculation is acceptable; otherwise add minimal read-only contract fields/handler logic in the standings feature. Do not introduce a separate global “prediction” subsystem.
- Compose existing MSS-141 zone/legend styling and backend contract; avoid broad rewrites or a new UI framework.
- Tests should use small synthetic point/remaining-stage fixtures or cheap existing templates, not full seasons solely to prove arithmetic.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Before Stage 32 completes, a mathematically uncatchable first-place athlete in any tier gets a clear **champion guaranteed** marker; a close contender with a reachable tie/overtake does not.
- [ ] Guaranteed direct promotion, qualifier challenger/defender, retention and direct relegation are identified only when **every** possible final rank remains inside that band's bounds.
- [ ] A top-four F1 athlete who could still finish first is not incorrectly labeled *guaranteed qualifier*; qualifying and direct advancement are not conflated.
- [ ] Boundary tests cover Superleague 16/17/24/25, F1 1/2/4/5/16/17/24/25, F2 8/9/16/17/24/25, F3 8/9/16/17 and inaugural F1 top four; every guarantee agrees with the season-specific zone table.
- [ ] Equal points/tie-break uncertainty, not-yet-complete stage, zero remaining stages, saved rules variants, malformed/missing official data, and stale cross-season/league responses cannot produce a false early guarantee.
- [ ] If zero stages remain, standings display ordinary final statuses rather than an “early” marker. Stage/matrix sorting never changes the athlete's proven status.
- [ ] Read-only guarantee computation leaves simulation results, save data and RNG unchanged; focused .NET/TypeScript tests prove correctness and consistency.
- [ ] On phone and desktop widths, user can distinguish **projected zone** from **guaranteed outcome** without relying only on color.

## Out of scope

- Predicting probabilities, plotting odds, simulating future stages or presenting “likely relegation” as guaranteed.
- Mathematical exact finishing-place projections when only a zone needs proof; exhaustive future-placement enumeration.
- Postseason qualifier results, guaranteed victories in qualifiers, Chaos/Cup formats, normal structural rebalancing or next-season roster forecasts.
- Any mutation to simulation RNG, championship points, tie-breaks, saved rules, results, historical records or movement logic.
- Redesign of league tables outside MSS-141's season standings page; broad dashboard/live/history changes or new stored lock-state schema.
