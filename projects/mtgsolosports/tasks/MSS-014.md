# MSS-014 — Create the inaugural Superleague after Season 1

## Goal

Implement the special Season 1 transition that selects the top four from each feeder league to create the first 32-athlete Superleague.

## Context

- Project: `mtgsolosports`.
- Target repository: `AlexBDevCorner/MtgSoloSports`.
- Default branch: `main`.
- Product rules: `docs/game-rules.md` in the target repository.
- Technical constraints: `docs/technical-design.md` and `AGENTS.md`.
- Architecture is vertical-slice modular monolith. **MediatR is forbidden.**
- Keep sporting simulation deterministic and use fixed-point integer arithmetic.

## Requirements

1. After Season 1 final feeder standings, select places 1-4 from each of eight leagues.
2. Create exactly 32 unique Season 2 Superleague members and remove them from their feeder membership.
3. Preserve all athlete bonus history; no bonus reset on promotion.
4. Do not fill feeder vacancies from the common pool until movement has been resolved by the appropriate rebalancing flow.
5. Persist promotion movement/history records and expose the inaugural Superleague roster.
6. Validate no athlete is active in two leagues and Superleague count is exactly 32.
7. Add deterministic transition tests.

## Architectural direction

- Treat this as an explicit special lifecycle slice, not conditionals scattered through normal promotion logic.
